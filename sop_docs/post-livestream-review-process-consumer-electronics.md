# Post-Livestream Review Process (Consumer Electronics)

## When to Use

Use this procedure after every Consumer Electronics campaign. The **review** must be completed within 48 hours after the campaign ends. The Electronics Data team owns this process.

## Procedure

1. **Prepare the review**: The Electronics Data team gathers the campaign results and any relevant notes before the review meeting.

2. **Review the core numbers**: Assess the conversion rate, demo-to-purchase rate, and returns marked “not as described.” Use these measures to identify results that need discussion.

3. **Review the product demonstrations**: Check whether the products shown and their specifications were presented accurately. Pay particular attention to any product spec shown on screen that was wrong.

4. **Hold the review meeting**: The host, the product specialist and the data analyst attend. Discuss the core numbers, demonstration accuracy and any issues that affected the campaign.

5. **Record the findings**: Enter the review findings in the Electronics review tracker. Include relevant context and any follow-up actions.

6. **Correct inaccurate specifications**: If a wrong product spec was shown on screen, correct it at the start of the next stream. Confirm that the corrected specification is displayed accurately.

7. **Close the review**: Check that findings and follow-up actions are recorded in the Electronics review tracker.

**Closing notes:** The review is complete when the findings are recorded and any required correction to an on-screen product specification is made at the start of the next stream.

## Partner Teams

- **Electronics Data team**: Owns the review process, coordinates the review and records findings in the Electronics review tracker.
- **Host**: Attends the review and provides context on how the campaign was presented.
- **Product specialist**: Attends the review, supports checks on product information and helps correct inaccurate specifications.
- **Data analyst**: Attends the review and presents the core numbers.
