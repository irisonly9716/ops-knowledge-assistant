# Sale Event Inventory Check (Beauty)

## When to Use

Use this procedure before every Beauty sale stream. The check is done 72 hours before the stream so the team has time to resolve stock, shelf-life, shade availability, and storage issues before products are presented to customers. Beauty inventory needs a product-level review: a total count can look adequate while specific lipstick or foundation shades are short, expired stock can be mixed with eligible stock, and testers or free samples can make sellable quantities appear higher than they are. The review also confirms that temperature-sensitive serums are stored correctly. These checks protect customers from products with too little shelf life, prevent unavailable shades from being offered, and give the stream team a reliable view of what it can sell. The Beauty category manager signs off the check after the required reviews are complete.

## Procedure

1. **Review timing and scope**: Start the check 72 hours before the stream. Confirm the sale list and review every product planned for sale. This timing gives the team a clear point to identify issues and coordinate any changes before the stream.

2. **Check shelf life**: Confirm the remaining shelf life for each product. Do not put any product with less than 6 months of shelf life left on sale. This rule keeps products with insufficient remaining shelf life out of the offer and reduces the risk of customer complaints.

3. **Check shade-level stock**: Review lipstick and foundation inventory shade by shade. Take a shade with fewer than 50 units out of the stream. A product-level total does not show whether each shade can support the offer, so each shade needs its own decision.

4. **Separate sellable stock**: Count testers and free samples separately from sellable stock. Do not include them in the quantity available for sale. This keeps the sale quantity accurate and prevents non-sellable items from being treated as customer inventory.

5. **Confirm storage and sign-off**: Check that temperature-sensitive serums are stored below 25°C. Record any issue for resolution before the stream, then send the completed check to the Beauty category manager for sign-off. Correct storage helps protect serum quality, and manager sign-off confirms that the inventory review is complete.

## Partner Teams

- **Beauty Supply Chain team**: Owns the check, reviews sale inventory, and coordinates resolution of inventory issues.
- **Warehouse & Logistics**: Confirms stock counts, separates testers and free samples from sellable stock, and verifies serum storage conditions.
- **Beauty category manager**: Reviews and signs off the completed check.
- **Livestream operations team**: Updates the stream plan when a shade or product is removed from the sale.
