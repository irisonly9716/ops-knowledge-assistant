# Campaign Review Writing Guidelines

Owner: Ops Analytics team
Applies to: Every department

## Scope

- Category: Review
- Applies company-wide to post-campaign reviews.
- Review due within 10 business days after the campaign ends.
- Required sections: results against targets, ROI, product breakdown, recommendations for similar campaigns.

## Steps

1. Pull figures from the uploaded data tables; never estimate numbers by hand.
2. Compare each result with its target range: inside the range is on target, below it is below target, above it is above target.
3. Calculate ROI as total product profit divided by actual operating cost.
4. Use product profit after cost of goods; do not subtract cost of goods again.
5. Compare actual operating cost with the plan.
6. If operating cost is more than 5% over plan, flag it in **bold** and explain the overage.
7. Write the product breakdown from the uploaded data tables.
8. Add recommendations for similar campaigns, based on the results and cost review.
9. Check that every required section is present before sharing the review.

## Contacts

- Ops Analytics team: review format, calculations, and data questions
- Campaign team: campaign details and recommendations for similar campaigns
