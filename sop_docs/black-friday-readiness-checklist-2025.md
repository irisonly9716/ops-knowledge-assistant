# Black Friday Readiness Checklist 2025

## Purpose

This company-wide launch checklist sets the readiness requirements for Black Friday 2025. Every department must complete the applicable steps and raise any readiness risk with the Ops team.

## Definitions

**Black Friday:** November 28, 2025, the date of the company’s Black Friday event.

**Black Friday week:** The week that includes Black Friday.

**War room:** The shared operations coordination space for managing Black Friday readiness and live issues.

**Inventory freeze:** The restriction on moving stock between warehouses.

## Procedure

1. **Confirm advertising budgets.**
   a. Each department must confirm that its advertising budget is approved by October 31, 2025.
   b. The responsible team must share confirmation with the Ops team. Raise any outstanding approval as a readiness risk.

2. **Finalize the stream schedule.**
   a. The stream schedule must be final by November 7, 2025.
   b. Departments involved in programming, staffing, or stream operations must confirm their commitments against the final schedule.

3. **Freeze inventory.**
   a. Inventory is frozen on November 14, 2025.
   b. No stock moves between warehouses after that date.
   c. Teams responsible for inventory and fulfilment must plan warehouse stock needs before the freeze and follow the restriction during Black Friday operations.

4. **Load test checkout.**
   a. The checkout is load tested on November 18, 2025.
   b. The teams responsible for checkout and technical operations must review the test results and raise any issue that could affect purchases with the Ops team.

5. **Prepare studio backup equipment.**
   a. New in 2025: a second backup stream encoder is required for every studio.
   b. Studio and stream operations teams must confirm that each studio has the encoder available and ready for use before Black Friday.

6. **Staff the war room.**
   a. The war room must be staffed from 8 am to midnight during Black Friday week.
   b. The Ops team must coordinate coverage with every department. Each department must identify the appropriate contact for readiness questions and live issues, and ensure that contact knows how to reach the war room.

7. **Confirm readiness.**
   a. Each department must review the requirements that apply to its work and confirm readiness with the Ops team.
   b. Report any incomplete requirement or operational risk promptly. The Ops team will coordinate follow-up with the responsible department.

## Notes

This checklist applies company-wide. Department leads are responsible for communicating relevant requirements to their teams and coordinating completion with the Ops team.
