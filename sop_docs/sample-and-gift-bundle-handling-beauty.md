# Sample and Gift Bundle Handling (Beauty)

## Overview

Owner: Beauty Operations team. Category: Fulfillment. This procedure covers samples and gifts included with Beauty orders. Follow the campaign plan for gift-with-purchase thresholds and use the steps below when packing, replacing, or processing a returned bundle.

## Questions and Answers

### How do I know whether an order qualifies for a gift?

Gift-with-purchase thresholds are set in the campaign plan. Check the plan before packing and include a gift only when the order meets the applicable threshold.

### How many samples can go in an order?

An order gets at most 5 samples. Do not add extra samples even if more are available.

### Are samples charged to the customer?

No. Samples are never charged for. Keep sample charges off the order.

### Where should gift stock be held?

Gift stock is reserved separately from sellable stock. Do not use reserved gift stock to fulfill regular product orders, or take sellable stock for gifts without the proper allocation.

### What should I do if a gift runs out?

Replace it with a gift of equal or higher value. The host announces the change so customers know which gift they will receive.

### What happens if a bundle is returned without its gift?

Deduct $5 per missing gift from the refund. Confirm that the gift is missing before applying the deduction, and record it with the return.

### What should I check before an order leaves fulfillment?

Confirm the order meets the campaign plan threshold, add no more than 5 samples, and make sure any gift comes from reserved gift stock. If the original gift is unavailable, use the approved equal-or-higher-value replacement and ensure the host announces the change.

## Escalation

Contact the Beauty Operations team if the campaign plan is unclear, reserved gift stock is unavailable, or you cannot confirm an eligible replacement.
