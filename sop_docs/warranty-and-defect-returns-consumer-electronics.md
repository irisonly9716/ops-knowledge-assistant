# Warranty and Defect Returns (Consumer Electronics)

Owner: Electronics After-Sales team
Applies to: Consumer Electronics department

## Scope

- Category: Customer Service
- Every product has a 12-month warranty
- Covers warranty claims for defective consumer electronics
- Refurbished units are never sold in a livestream

## Steps

1. Customer contacts the team with the order details and defect description.
2. Ask the customer to send a video that shows the defect.
3. Confirm the product and warranty status before approving the return.
4. For a unit that is dead on arrival within 7 days: replace it, do not repair it.
5. For other covered defects: arrange the return and assess the unit.
6. If repair is appropriate, complete it within at most 10 business days.
7. Update the customer on the outcome and return or replacement arrangements.
8. Keep refurbished units out of livestream inventory.

## Contacts

- Electronics After-Sales team: warranty claims, defect review, repairs, and replacements
- Customer Service: customer updates and return coordination
