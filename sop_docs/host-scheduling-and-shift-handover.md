# Host Scheduling and Shift Handover

Owner: Live Operations team
Applies to: Every department

## Scope

- Company-wide host scheduling and shift handovers
- Applies to all departments that support livestreams
- Use this process for regular shifts and approved swaps

## Steps

1. Live Operations publishes the schedule for the next week every Thursday.
2. Check your assigned shifts and raise scheduling issues with Live Operations.
3. Keep each host on camera for at most 4 hours a day.
4. Arrange a 15-minute overlap between two hosts at each handover.
5. Before the outgoing host leaves, update the handover sheet with stock status, promises made in the chat that are still open, and any complaints.
6. Review the handover sheet with the incoming host during the overlap.
7. For a shift swap, give 48 hours' notice and get confirmation from the Live Operations team before treating the swap as approved.
8. If a swap is not confirmed, follow the published schedule and contact Live Operations about coverage.
9. Flag missing handover information or unresolved issues to the incoming host and Live Operations.

## Contacts

- Live Operations team: schedule, shift swaps, coverage, and handover issues
- Department lead: department-specific support and escalation
