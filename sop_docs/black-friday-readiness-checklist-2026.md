# Black Friday Readiness Checklist 2026

## Purpose

This policy sets company-wide readiness requirements for Black Friday 2026. Every department must complete its assigned work by the deadlines below.

## Definitions

**Black Friday 2026** — November 27, 2026, the date of the company’s Black Friday event.

**Seasonal campaign budget** — The approved advertising budget for seasonal campaigns, including the required buffer for rising ad bids.

**War room** — The staffed coordination point for Black Friday week operations.

## Procedure

1. **Approve advertising budgets.**
   a. The Ops team coordinates approval of advertising budgets by October 23, 2026.
   b. This deadline is a week earlier than in 2025 because seasonal campaigns ran over budget.
   c. Seasonal campaign budgets must include a 10% buffer for rising ad bids.

2. **Finalize the stream schedule.**
   a. Each department submits its scheduling requirements to Ops.
   b. The stream schedule must be final by November 6, 2026. Ops shares the approved schedule with all departments.

3. **Freeze inventory.**
   a. Inventory is frozen on November 13, 2026.
   b. Departments must raise inventory risks with Ops before the freeze so they can be reviewed and resolved.

4. **Lock prices.**
   a. Prices are locked 48 hours before each Black Friday stream, instead of the usual 24 hours.
   b. The responsible department confirms each stream’s prices are accurate before the lock. Changes after the lock require escalation to Ops.

5. **Staff the war room.**
   a. The war room is staffed from 7 am to 1 am during Black Friday week.
   b. Each department provides an available contact and escalates operational issues to the war room for coordination.

6. **Confirm readiness.**
   a. Department leads verify that their teams have completed the applicable steps and know the approved schedule.
   b. Report unresolved risks to Ops promptly and keep the war room informed during Black Friday week.

## Notes

This checklist applies to every department. Ops owns coordination and follow-up; each department remains responsible for its assigned work and for raising risks that could affect readiness.
