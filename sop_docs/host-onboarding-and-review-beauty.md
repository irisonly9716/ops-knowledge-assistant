# Host Onboarding and Review (Beauty)

## When to Use

Use this procedure when the Beauty Live team onboards a new host or conducts a monthly review. It covers skincare and color cosmetics readiness, trial streams, and the host’s probation period.

## Procedure

1. **Set up onboarding**: Confirm the host’s product focus and explain the onboarding process. Assign a Beauty Live team contact for questions and support.

2. **Complete skincare training and quiz**: Cover skincare products, common customer questions, and how to describe products accurately. The host must pass the skincare knowledge quiz with a score of 80% before presenting skincare products.

3. **Complete shade-matching training**: Every new host must complete shade-matching training before streaming color cosmetics. Review how to assess and explain shade options, and how to help customers choose a suitable match.

4. **Run trial streams**: Schedule 3 trial streams, each lasting 1 hour. Have an experienced team member observe each stream and give the host clear feedback on product knowledge, presentation, and customer questions.

5. **Start probation**: Probation lasts 60 days. During this period, provide regular coaching and address gaps in product knowledge or stream execution. Confirm that required training is complete before assigning relevant product streams.

6. **Review performance monthly**: Compare the host’s conversion rate with the department average. Check the host’s return rate; it should stay below 15%. Discuss results with the host, identify any issues, and agree on follow-up actions. Consider product fit, customer questions, and presentation when reviewing the results.

7. **Record the review**: Document the review discussion, performance observations, and agreed actions in the host’s profile. Share relevant follow-up needs with the team members supporting the host.

## Partner Teams

- **Beauty Live team**: Coordinates onboarding, trial streams, coaching, and monthly reviews.
- **Training team**: Supports skincare knowledge and shade-matching training.
- **Data team**: Provides conversion-rate and return-rate information for monthly reviews.
