# Bundle Pricing Rules (Home Goods)

## Overview

Owner: Home Goods Merchandising team. Category: Pricing.
These rules apply to bundles offered by the Home Goods department. Check each bundle against them before it is listed or repriced.

## Questions and Answers

### How many items can a bundle include?

A bundle must contain 2 to 4 items. Do not list a bundle with fewer or more items.

### What discount can we offer?

The bundle discount must be at most 25%. Any discount above 15% requires approval from the Pricing team before the bundle is offered.

### Does every bundle need a high-margin item?

Yes. Every bundle must include at least one item with a margin above 35%. Confirm the margin for an item before including it to meet this rule.

### Can we bundle clearance items?

No. Clearance items are never bundled. Do not add a clearance item to a bundle, even if the other items meet the bundle rules.

### What should I check before listing a bundle?

Confirm the item count, discount, and margin requirements, and make sure none of the items are clearance. If the discount is above 15%, obtain Pricing team approval before listing. Keep the bundle details and approval information with the pricing record.

### What if an item’s status or margin is unclear?

Hold the bundle until the item’s status or margin is confirmed. Do not assume an item is eligible when its clearance status or margin has not been verified.

## Escalation

Contact the Pricing team for approval when a proposed discount is above 15%, or when you need guidance on applying these rules. Keep the bundle off offer until the approval or guidance is received.
