# Customer Complaint Escalation

## Purpose

This policy sets the escalation process for customer complaints across every department. The Customer Service team owns the process and is responsible for maintaining a complete complaint record.

## Definitions

**Complaint:** A customer expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, order or company interaction.

**Complaint ID:** The identifier assigned to a complaint in the customer relationship management system (CRM).

**Public comment:** A complaint or response posted where other customers or the public can view it.

**Private message:** A direct communication visible only to the customer and the company.

## Procedure

1. **Log every complaint.**
   a. Record each complaint in the CRM and include its complaint ID.
   b. Add the customer’s concern, relevant communications and actions taken. Keep the record factual and update it as the complaint progresses.

2. **Handle Level 1 complaints.**
   a. A support agent must answer the complaint within 4 hours.
   b. The agent should acknowledge the concern, clarify any information needed, and explain the next action. The agent remains responsible for the complaint unless it meets a higher escalation level.

3. **Escalate to Level 2 when required.**
   a. A team lead takes over if the complaint is unresolved after 24 hours.
   b. A team lead also takes over if the customer threatens a public review, whether or not 24 hours have passed.
   c. The team lead reviews the CRM record, coordinates the response, and keeps the customer informed until the complaint is resolved or escalated again.

4. **Escalate to Level 3 when required.**
   a. Trust & Safety and Legal take over for any complaint involving an injury, a product safety issue or a regulator’s inquiry.
   b. Notify both teams promptly and provide the CRM record and relevant supporting information. Do not make commitments or speculate about responsibility while those teams review the matter.
   c. Continue to preserve related communications and follow the direction of Trust & Safety and Legal.

5. **Keep compensation private.**
   a. Compensation is never discussed in public comments. Discuss it only in a private message.
   b. If a customer raises compensation in a public comment, acknowledge the concern without discussing compensation and invite the customer to continue by private message.

## Notes

This policy applies company-wide, including to employees who encounter complaints outside the Customer Service team. Route complaints to Customer Service for logging and handling. Do not delete or alter complaint records to make an issue appear resolved; record corrections and follow-up actions in the CRM.
