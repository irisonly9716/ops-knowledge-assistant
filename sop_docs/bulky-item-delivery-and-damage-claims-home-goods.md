# Bulky Item Delivery and Damage Claims (Home Goods)

## Overview

Owner: Home Goods Logistics team. Category: Fulfillment. This procedure covers freight delivery and damage claims for bulky Home Goods items.

## Questions and Answers

### Which items ship by freight?

Items over 30 lb ship by freight. Use the freight delivery process for these items rather than standard parcel delivery.

### How is the delivery window set?

The delivery window is booked with the customer. Confirm the agreed window and share the delivery details with the customer before the shipment is dispatched.

### What should I tell the customer before delivery?

Let the customer know that the delivery window is booked with them and ask them to check the carton and item when the delivery arrives. If the carton or item is damaged, they should contact us and provide photos of both.

### How soon does the customer need to make a damage claim?

A damage claim is made within 48 hours. Ask the customer to include photos of the carton and of the item so the team can review the damage.

### What photos do we need?

We need photos of the carton and of the item. Make sure the claim includes both, even when the damage to the item appears minor.

### How do we handle cosmetic damage?

Cosmetic damage gets a partial refund of 20%. Confirm that the damage is cosmetic and record the claim details before arranging the refund.

### What if the damage stops the item from working?

Damage that stops the item from working gets a replacement. Record the claim and retain the photos of the carton and item with the case.

## Escalation

Send the claim to the Home Goods Logistics team if the damage is unclear, the required photos are missing, or the issue does not fit either outcome above. Keep the customer updated while the team reviews the case.
