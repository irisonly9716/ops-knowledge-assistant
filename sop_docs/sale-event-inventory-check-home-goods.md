# Sale Event Inventory Check (Home Goods)

## When to Use

Use this procedure to check Home Goods inventory for a sale event livestream. Complete the check 7 days before the stream. Confirm that forecast stock, warehouse space, and product condition are ready before inventory is committed to the event.

## Procedure

1. **Review the forecast**: Confirm the expected demand for each product with the event team. Keep stock 10% above the forecast to provide a buffer for livestream orders.

2. **Confirm available stock**: Compare the forecast and buffer with inventory records. Flag any shortage or mismatch for review before the event stock is reserved.
   - For a multi-pack, count the pack as a single SKU.
   - Confirm that each product’s stock is available for the event and not already committed elsewhere.

3. **Check bulky-item space**: Confirm warehouse space for every bulky item in the event. Do not treat an item as ready until the warehouse confirms the space is available.

4. **Inspect cartons**: Open 5% of cartons and check the contents for damage. Record any damage and flag affected stock so it is not treated as ready for the stream.

5. **Resolve exceptions**: Review shortages, stock mismatches, damage, and space concerns with the relevant partner teams. Confirm the resolution before finalizing the event inventory.

6. **Reserve event stock**: Once checks are complete, reserve the approved event stock in the inventory system. Share any remaining exceptions with the event team so they can adjust the product plan.

## Partner Teams

- **Warehouse & Logistics**: Confirm warehouse space for bulky items, support stock checks, and help resolve inventory or carton damage issues.
- **Livestream Operations**: Confirm the event forecast and product plan, and review any inventory exceptions that could affect the stream.
- **Home Goods Supply Chain team**: Own the inventory check, coordinate resolutions, and confirm when event stock is ready.
