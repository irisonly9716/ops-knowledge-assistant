# Promotional Pricing and Discount Approval

## Purpose

This policy sets the approval requirements for promotional prices and discounts across every department. It applies to all products and bundles sold through company livestreams.

## Definitions

**Promotional price** — The price offered during a promotion or livestream.

**Discount** — The reduction from the product’s regular price, expressed as a percentage.

**Bundle discount** — The combined discount applied to a bundle, assessed for the bundle as a whole rather than separately for each item.

**Cost price** — The company’s cost of a product.

## Procedure

1. Determine the discount and applicable approver before setting or advertising a promotional price.
   a. A discount of up to 15% requires approval from the department manager.
   b. A discount between 15% and 30% requires approval from the Pricing team.
   c. A discount above 30% requires approval from the finance director.
   d. Obtain the required approval before communicating or applying the promotional price.

2. Check the proposed promotional price against the product’s cost price.
   a. No promotional price may go below the product’s cost price.
   b. Do not apply or advertise a price that fails this check.

3. Apply the approval rules to bundles.
   a. Assess a bundle discount as the combined discount of the bundle, not as a discount per item.
   b. Route the combined discount to the approver required for that discount.

4. Lock prices ahead of each stream.
   a. Prices are locked 24 hours before a stream.
   b. After the lock, make no price change without approval from the Pricing team.
   c. Obtain that approval before changing or communicating the revised price.

5. Keep a record of pricing decisions.
   a. Record the proposed price, discount, relevant product or bundle, approver, and approval.
   b. Make the record available to the Pricing team when requested.

## Notes

This policy applies company-wide, regardless of department or sales channel used for the livestream. Department managers, the Pricing team, and the finance director must review requests according to the approval thresholds above. A price lock does not waive the cost-price restriction or the required discount approval.
