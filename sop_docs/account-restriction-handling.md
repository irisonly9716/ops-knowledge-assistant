# Account Restriction Handling

## When to Use

A livestream room or shop account receives a platform restriction notice (reduced reach, suspension, disabled features, orders flagged as abnormal), or orders are held because of customer complaints. Applies to every department.

## Procedure

1. **Identify the rule and the account**: Log in to the platform's seller center and note the exact policy that was triggered, when it happened, and which livestream or product is involved. Take screenshots as evidence.
2. **Check whether we caused it**: Review the stream for prohibited claims, misleading product descriptions or signs of fake orders. If the cause is on our side, fix it first and appeal afterwards.
3. **Collect appeal evidence**: The stream recording around the flagged moment (5 minutes before and after), product certificates and shipping records. The more specific the evidence, the higher the chance the appeal succeeds.
4. **Submit the appeal ticket**: File a ticket in the seller center with the evidence from step 3, the account ID, the livestream ID and what we are asking for.
5. **Notify the Trust & Safety contact**: After filing, mention the on-call contact in the Trust & Safety channel and say how urgent it is (whether today's livestream is affected).
6. **Wait for the review**: It usually takes 1-3 business days. Do not file the same ticket again; duplicates push it further back in the queue.
7. **Follow up on the result**:
   - Appeal approved: confirm every feature is restored, save screenshots and add the case to the department knowledge base.
   - Appeal rejected: ask the Trust & Safety contact for the exact reason and decide whether to escalate to the Trust & Safety lead.

## Partner Teams

- **Trust & Safety team**: first point of contact; reviews appeals
- **Legal** (for certificate or compliance questions): helps prepare compliance documents
- **Engineering** (when features are broken but the seller center shows no restriction): checks whether it is a system bug rather than a real restriction
