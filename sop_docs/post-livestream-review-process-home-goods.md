# Post-Livestream Review Process (Home Goods)

## When to Use

Use this process for the Home Goods livestream review. Streams are reviewed together once a week, every Monday, covering the week before. Do not review streams one by one. The Home Goods Data team owns the process.

## Procedure

1. **Prepare the review**: Gather the records for the streams from the week before. Check that the host and merchandiser can attend, and share the material they will need to discuss the results.

2. **Review the core numbers**: Look at average order value, bundle attach rate and damage-in-transit complaints. Use these numbers to focus the discussion on order value, product combinations and delivery damage. Note any questions that need follow-up.

3. **Discuss the stream together**: Review the week’s streams as a group rather than treating each stream as a separate review. Ask the host and merchandiser to share what they observed, including which product presentations or bundles worked well and where customers had concerns.

4. **Record findings and follow-up**: Capture the key observations, the core numbers and any agreed actions in the review record. Assign each action to the team or person responsible, and note what information is still needed.

   - If damage-in-transit complaints came up, record the relevant issue and pass it to the team that can investigate delivery or packaging.
   - If the discussion identifies a product or bundle question, record the details for the merchandiser to follow up.
   - If the host identifies a presentation issue, record it for the appropriate host support team.

5. **Share and retain the review**: Send the summary and follow-up actions to the people involved. Keep the record with the Home Goods review materials so the team can refer to it in later weekly discussions.

## Partner Teams

- **Host Management team**: Helps with host observations, presentation questions and follow-up support.
- **Merchandising team**: Helps assess product presentation, bundle choices and product-related questions.
- **Delivery or packaging team**: Helps investigate damage-in-transit complaints and related follow-up.
