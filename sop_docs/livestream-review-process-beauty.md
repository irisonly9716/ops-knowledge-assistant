# Post-Livestream Review Process (Beauty)

## When to Use

The Beauty department reviews every major campaign (expected GMV above $50,000, or running for three days or more) within 48 hours after it ends.

## Procedure

1. **Pull the core numbers**: GMV, DAU, average watch time and conversion rate, compared with the same period of the previous campaign in the category.
2. **Break it down by hour**: Look at the hourly traffic curve, mark the hours with unusually high or low conversion, and replay the host's script and product lineup at those times.
3. **Analyze by product**: Which SKUs drove most of the GMV, which ones were listed but never converted, and whether to keep scheduling them.
4. **Analyze by host**: Compare lead capture and conversion with the host's historical average to judge whether this was a normal performance.
5. **Log incidents**: Record any platform restrictions, equipment failures or complaints, how long they took to resolve and what they affected, and pass them to the team that owns the matching SOP.
6. **Hold the review meeting**: The data team prepares a one-page summary; host, ops and supply chain meet for 30 minutes and agree on 2-3 concrete improvements for next time.
7. **Archive**: Store the review document in the department's shared library, tagged with the campaign_id, so the next similar campaign can find it.

## Partner Teams

- **Host Management team**: the host's condition and anything unusual on the day
- **Supply Chain**: outside factors that hurt conversion, such as stockouts or shipping delays
