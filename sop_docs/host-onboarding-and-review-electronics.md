# Host Onboarding and Review (Consumer Electronics)

## When to Use

The Consumer Electronics department signs a new host, or runs the quarterly review of existing hosts.

## Procedure

1. **Background check**: Confirm that the host's past product categories and audience profile fit consumer electronics buyers, and check for past policy violations.
2. **Trial streams**: Schedule 1-2 trial streams alongside an experienced host (never solo) and watch how well they know product specs and how they handle surprises.
3. **Confirm the contract terms**: Placement fee or commission rate, minimum number of streams and review period; Legal reviews the contract template.
4. **Product training**: Electronics needs real product knowledge, so schedule at least 4 hours of product training plus a written test. No solo streams until the test is passed.
5. **First-month support**: An experienced ops lead joins the first 4 streams, reviews each one's numbers and corrects problems early.
6. **Quarterly review**: Compare GMV, conversion rate, return rate and complaint rate with the department average. Falling below the threshold triggers three escalation levels: "check-in -> probation -> termination".
7. **File the review**: Store the review form in the host's profile; it sets their scheduling priority next quarter.

## Partner Teams

- **Legal**: contract review
- **Trust & Safety team**: checks past policy violations
- **Data team**: provides the quarterly review numbers
