# Product Demo Setup for Livestreams (Consumer Electronics)

## Purpose

This procedure sets the requirements for preparing and demonstrating consumer electronics during livestreams. It applies to all demo products used by the Consumer Electronics department.

## Definitions

**Demo unit**
The product selected for use in a livestream demonstration.

**Backup unit**
A second unit of the same product, available if the demo unit cannot be used.

**Screen mirroring**
Displaying a device’s screen on the livestream production display or stream.

## Procedure

1. **Select and prepare demo units.**
   a. Confirm the product matches the livestream plan and is in working condition.
   b. Ensure all demo units are fully charged and running the latest firmware before the stream.
   c. Set up a backup unit for every product that is demonstrated. Confirm it is charged, functional, and ready to use.

2. **Prepare the demonstration area.**
   a. Place each product and its accessories within the host’s reach.
   b. Keep cables and other equipment organized so they do not obstruct the host or appear as a safety hazard.
   c. Confirm products are clean and positioned so viewers can see relevant features.

3. **Test connectivity and presentation.**
   a. Test screen mirroring 30 minutes before the stream. Confirm the correct device is connected and its display is visible in the stream.
   b. Check that any required connections, controls, and demonstration content work as intended.
   c. If screen mirroring or another essential function fails, troubleshoot before the stream. Use the backup unit when appropriate and notify the production lead if the issue remains unresolved.

4. **Conduct product demonstrations.**
   a. Demonstrate product features accurately and follow the livestream plan.
   b. Hosts never compare the product with a competitor’s by name. Keep comparisons focused on the product’s own features and intended use.
   c. Demonstrate earbuds and speakers below 85 dB. Monitor the sound level during the demonstration and lower it if it reaches the limit.
   d. Do not continue a demonstration if the product becomes unsafe, stops working, or cannot be presented accurately. Switch to the backup unit or inform the production lead.

5. **Complete the setup check.**
   a. Confirm the demo unit and backup unit are ready and accessible.
   b. Confirm the host understands the demonstration plan and the requirements in this procedure.
   c. Report unresolved equipment or setup issues to the production lead before the stream begins.

## Notes

The Electronics Live team owns this procedure and is responsible for maintaining consistent setup practices. When a product or feature cannot be demonstrated as planned, the host should describe the limitation clearly and avoid unsupported claims.
