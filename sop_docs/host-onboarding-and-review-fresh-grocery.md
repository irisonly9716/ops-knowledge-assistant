# Host Onboarding and Review (Fresh Grocery)

Owner: Fresh Grocery Live team
Applies to: Fresh Grocery hosts

## When to Use

- New host joining Fresh Grocery Live.
- Host review during or after probation.
- Use for onboarding, stream readiness and performance follow-up.

## Procedure

1. **Food handling training**: Every new host completes food handling training before hosting.
2. **Trial streams**: Schedule 2 trial streams; review product handling and on-camera practice.
3. **Raw products**: Never taste raw meat or raw seafood on camera. Stop the tasting and redirect to a safe product or preparation discussion.
4. **Probation**: Place each new host on probation for 45 days.
5. **Review**: Check the spoilage complaint rate and the conversion rate. Consider both when reviewing performance.
6. **Follow-up**: Share the review with the host; document any concerns and next steps in the host’s record.

## Partner Teams

- **Food Safety**: Provides food handling training and guidance on safe product demonstrations.
- **Fresh Grocery Live team**: Schedules trial streams, reviews host performance and records follow-up.
- **Data team**: Provides spoilage complaint rate and conversion rate data.
