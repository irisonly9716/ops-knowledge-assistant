# Sale Event Inventory Check (Consumer Electronics)

## When to Use

Use this procedure for Consumer Electronics products scheduled for a sale-event stream. The **check** must be completed 5 days before the stream. The Electronics Supply Chain team owns the check and records the result for each product and batch.

## Procedure

1. **Identify sale inventory**: Confirm the products and batches allocated to the stream with the inventory record and the warehouse team. Keep any unconfirmed stock out of the sale allocation until its status is clear.

2. **Verify units and serial numbers**:
   a. Scan every unit’s serial number and match the scan to the product and batch record.
   b. Resolve missing, unreadable, duplicate, or mismatched serial numbers before marking a unit as available.
   c. Record exceptions and keep affected units separate from sale-ready inventory.

3. **Confirm firmware consistency**: Check the firmware version on the units for each product. All units of a product must run the same firmware version. If versions differ, hold the affected units and coordinate with the responsible team to bring the product’s units to a consistent version before release.

4. **Review batch quality history**: Check the defect rate for the previous batch of each product. If the previous batch had a defect rate above 2%, hold the current batch for inspection. Do not release that batch for the stream until the inspection is complete and its disposition is recorded.

5. **Check air-shipping restrictions**: Identify power banks in the sale inventory and confirm their watt-hour rating. Power banks above 100 Wh can’t ship by air. Flag those units for an appropriate shipping plan and do not assign them to air transport.

6. **Record the release decision**:
   a. Mark each product and batch as approved, held, or requiring follow-up.
   b. Record serial-number exceptions, firmware issues, inspection holds, and shipping restrictions with the relevant inventory record.
   c. Share unresolved issues with the partner teams responsible for action. Release only inventory that has passed the applicable checks.

7. **Closing notes**: Keep the check record with the sale-event inventory documentation. Update it when a hold is cleared or an exception is resolved, and notify the stream operations team of any inventory that remains unavailable.

## Partner Teams

- **Warehouse & Logistics**: confirms allocated stock, separates held units, and plans shipping for power banks that cannot travel by air.
- **Quality team**: inspects a batch held because the previous batch had a defect rate above 2%.
- **Device Support team**: helps resolve firmware-version differences.
- **Stream Operations team**: receives the final availability status and any remaining inventory restrictions.
