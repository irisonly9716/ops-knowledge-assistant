# Post-Livestream Review Process (Fresh Grocery)

## When to Use

Use this procedure after every Fresh Grocery livestream that includes perishable products. The review happens the morning after the stream, by 10 am, while the details of the broadcast and the condition of the stock are still available to the people who need to act on them. Fresh products have a limited window for sale, so waiting to review the stream can leave less time to address complaints, understand product performance, and find another route for stock that did not sell. This review is not only a record of what happened. It is a working discussion that connects customer feedback and sell-through to decisions about product handling and remaining inventory. The host and the supply chain planner attend so that the team can consider both what customers experienced during the stream and what options are available for the stock afterward.

## Procedure

1. **Prepare the core numbers**: The Fresh Grocery Data team prepares the spoilage complaint rate and the sell-through of each perishable product. Use these measures to identify customer concerns and understand which products sold through and which remain. Looking at every perishable product separately helps the team avoid hiding a problem with one item inside an overall result.

2. **Review customer feedback and the stream**: Discuss spoilage complaints in the context of what customers saw and heard during the livestream. The host explains how products were presented and responds to any questions about what happened on air. This discussion helps distinguish issues with product condition from issues with how the product or its handling was explained.

3. **Review product sell-through**: Go through sell-through for each perishable product with the supply chain planner. Identify products with stock left over and discuss what affected their sale, including relevant supply or availability context. Product-level review gives the team a clear basis for handling remaining stock rather than relying on a general impression of the stream.

4. **Agree on stock actions**: Move perishable stock left over to other channels within 24 hours. Agree on the next channel and the handoff needed to make the move. The time limit exists because perishable stock can lose value or become unsuitable for sale if action is delayed.

5. **Record the review**: The Fresh Grocery Data team records the core numbers, the main discussion points, and the agreed stock actions. A clear record lets the department follow through on decisions and refer to the review when preparing future livestreams.

## Partner Teams

The **Fresh Grocery Data team** owns the review and prepares the core numbers, then records the discussion and agreed actions.

The **host** attends the review and provides context on product presentation, customer questions, and the stream.

The **supply chain planner** attends the review, provides supply context, and helps coordinate the move of perishable stock left over to other channels within 24 hours.
