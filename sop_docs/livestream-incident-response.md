# Livestream Incident Response

## Purpose

This procedure applies company-wide to livestream incidents, including stream interruptions, pricing errors, product sellouts, and offensive comments. The Live Operations team owns this procedure.

## Definitions

**Incident:** An event during a livestream that requires action under this procedure.

**Producer:** The person responsible for the stream setup and backup stream.

**Moderator:** The person responsible for managing comments during the stream.

**Incident log:** The record of the incident, actions taken, and relevant follow-up.

## Procedure

1. **Respond to a dropped stream.**
   a. The producer switches to the backup stream within 2 minutes of the stream dropping.
   b. While the producer switches streams, the host keeps talking and gives viewers a brief update.

2. **Respond to a wrong price on screen.**
   a. Remove the product link at once.
   b. Review orders placed at the wrong price. Honor orders if the error is less than 30% off the right price. Otherwise, cancel the orders and provide an apology coupon of $10.
   c. The host should give a brief, accurate update and avoid confirming an order outcome before it is reviewed.

3. **Respond to a product that sells out mid-stream.**
   a. The host announces that the product has sold out and moves to the next product.
   b. Do not promise a waitlist on air.

4. **Respond to a raid of offensive comments.**
   a. The moderator turns on slow mode and blocks the accounts posting offensive comments.
   b. The host does not respond to the comments and continues the stream.

5. **Document and follow up.**
   a. The responsible Live Operations staff member files the incident log within 1 hour after the stream ends.
   b. Send a short post-incident note the next day. Include a concise summary of what happened and the actions taken.

## Notes

Apply this procedure across every department. When an incident involves more than one department, Live Operations coordinates the response and follow-up.
