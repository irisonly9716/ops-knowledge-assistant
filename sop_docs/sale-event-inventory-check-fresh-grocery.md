# Sale Event Inventory Check (Fresh Grocery)

## When to Use

Inventory checks before a major Fresh Grocery sale (Black Friday, the holiday sale, the anniversary sale). Fresh products need tighter timing and more accurate stock than any other category.

## Procedure

1. **Forecast demand**: Estimate expected sales from the GMV and DAU of similar past campaigns and the promotion's expected reach.
2. **Confirm supplier capacity**: Seven days ahead, confirm with suppliers that their capacity covers the forecast; for fresh products, also confirm cold-chain shipping capacity.
3. **Check shelf life**: Every product on sale must stay within its shelf life until at least three days after the campaign ends, to avoid complaints about near-expiry goods.
4. **Sync the inventory system**: Reserve the campaign stock in the system so other channels cannot oversell it and livestream orders never hit an empty shelf.
5. **Prepare warehouse and cold chain**: For fresh products, confirm cold-storage capacity and that shipping times can handle the order spike during the sale.
6. **Plan for problem SKUs**: If a SKU's stock looks wrong after the campaign starts (far above or below forecast), decide who pulls it or restocks it, and how fast they must respond.
7. **Count after the campaign**: Compare actual shipments with the system's stock records; investigate large differences (see the post-livestream review process).

## Partner Teams

- **Supplier Relations team**: capacity and cold-chain confirmation
- **Warehouse & Logistics**: stock reservation and shipping times
- **Trust & Safety team**: food compliance checks
