# Host Onboarding and Review (Home Goods)

## When to Use

Use this procedure when the Home Goods Live team onboards a new host or reviews a host during probation. It covers product practice, trial streams, and review of the host's performance.

## Procedure

1. **Prepare the host**: Explain the Home Goods product range, livestream expectations, and how the onboarding and review process works. Confirm the host knows where to find product information and whom to contact with questions.

2. **Practise product demos**: Have the new host practise clear, accurate demonstrations of Home Goods products. Practice should include assembling storage bins and using a spin mop. Review how the host explains product features and answers viewer questions.

3. **Run trial streams**: Schedule 2 trial streams of 90 minutes each. Review each stream for product knowledge, demonstration quality, audience engagement, and ability to handle questions. Share feedback after each stream and identify any areas the host should improve.

4. **Review performance**: Compare the host's average order value with the department average. Discuss the results with the host alongside feedback on product demonstrations and viewer interactions. Agree on practical steps to address any gaps.

5. **Manage probation**: Probation lasts 90 days. During probation, review the host's performance and provide coaching on any identified issues. At the end of probation, assess the review results and determine whether the host is ready to continue hosting for Home Goods.

6. **Record the outcome**: Document trial-stream feedback, the average order value comparison, coaching, and the probation decision in the host's record. Share relevant next steps with the host and the teams supporting their work.

## Partner Teams

- **Home Goods Live team**: Owns onboarding, product practice, stream feedback, and probation reviews.
- **Data team**: Provides the department average used to compare average order value.
- **Training or product team**: Helps confirm product details and supports accurate demonstrations.
- **Operations team**: Coordinates trial streams, scheduling, and host records.
