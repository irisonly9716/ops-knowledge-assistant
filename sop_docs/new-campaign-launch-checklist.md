# New Campaign Launch Checklist

## When to Use

Before a new campaign goes live (a seasonal sale, a product launch, a regular weekly stream). A self-check list shared by every department.

## Procedure

1. **Set up the campaign in the system**: Enter the campaign name, owning department, category, start and end dates and budget in the ops console.
2. **Confirm the host's schedule**: Check that the host has no conflicting booking and that a backup host is confirmed.
3. **Verify product compliance**: Every product in the campaign has its certificates in place (beauty and food products need the relevant permits), and every product link can be ordered.
4. **Check coupons and prices**: Discounts, stock levels and prices match the product pages, so there is no pricing incident on air.
5. **Test the streaming setup**: Test the network, equipment and lighting one hour before going live, so the stream does not start with a technical failure.
6. **Look at past results**: Check GMV, DAU and conversion rate of similar past campaigns and judge whether this campaign's targets are realistic.
7. **Run a policy self-check**: Go through the host script against the Trust & Safety list of restricted phrases, so the stream is not throttled right at the start.
8. **Confirm the contingency plan**: Know whom to call for an equipment failure, a network outage or a platform restriction (see the matching SOPs).

## Partner Teams

- **Host Management team**: host schedules and script review
- **Supply Chain / Warehouse**: stock checks
- **Trust & Safety team**: restricted phrases and compliance pre-check
- **Engineering**: equipment and streaming support
