# Home Back to School - Post-Campaign Review

## Results vs Targets

The campaign met its GMV and peak concurrent viewer targets, but conversion rate was below target. Operating cost was 8.9% over budget, beyond the 5% flag threshold.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $190K-$270K | $245.9K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 8%-10% | 7.87% | Below target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 1,500-3,500 | 3,187 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $47.9K | Actual $52.2K | **8.9% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

## ROI

An ROI of 1.79 means the campaign generated $1.79 in product profit for each $1 of operating cost.

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$93.6K**
- Actual operating cost: **$52.2K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 93.6 / 52.2 ≈ 1.79**

## Product Breakdown

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Laundry Detergent | 1,334 | $88.2K | $35.6K | 40.38% | 18.6% |
| Storage Bins | 368 | $52.6K | $17.6K | 33.46% | 2.6% |
| Spin Mop | 448 | $39.8K | $22.1K | 55.41% | 15.4% |
| Trash Bags | 166 | $17.8K | $10.3K | 57.80% | 19.2% |
| Hanger Set | 123 | $17.1K | $8.0K | 46.94% | 14.7% |

**Findings**:

1. **Laundry Detergent led sales and profit.** It sold 1,334 units, with $88.2K in sales and $35.6K in profit. Its 18.6% return rate is worth reviewing before the next campaign.
2. **Spin Mop had the strongest margin among the top sellers.** Its 55.41% margin and $22.1K in profit came with a 15.4% return rate.
3. **Trash Bags had the highest margin and return rate.** Margin was 57.80%, while the return rate was 19.2%. Check return reasons before featuring it again.
4. **Storage Bins had the lowest return rate.** Its 2.6% return rate was lower than every other product’s, alongside $52.6K in sales and $17.6K in profit.

## Recommendations for Similar Campaigns

- Review the reasons for returns on Laundry Detergent, Spin Mop, and Trash Bags before setting the next product lineup.
- Keep Storage Bins in consideration as a lower-return product, and assess whether its sales and profit support a larger role.
- Review operating cost drivers and plan a budget buffer for similar campaigns.
- Test changes to product demonstrations and offers to address the below-target conversion rate.
