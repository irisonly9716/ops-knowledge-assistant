# Home New Product Launch (Jan 8, 2026) - Post-Campaign Review

## How It Went

The Home New Product Launch ran from Jan 8, 2026 to Feb 16, 2026, with Lisa Hernandez hosting across 40 streamed days. The campaign finished with strong sales and conversion, and the live audience stayed within the target range at peak. The results suggest the product demonstrations gave viewers enough reason to move from watching to buying. Operating cost also came in below plan, so the campaign delivered those results without using the full budget. The product mix contributed in different ways: Storage Bins led sales, Spin Mop delivered the highest product profit, and Hanger Set moved the most units. Returns were lowest for Spin Mop and highest for Hanger Set, making return rate an important distinction when reviewing the unit volume. Overall, the campaign met its audience goal while exceeding its commercial targets and keeping costs controlled.

The strongest overall signal was the combination of above-target conversion and lower-than-planned operating cost.

## Results

GMV and conversion rate finished above target, while peak concurrent viewers landed on target. Actual operating cost was below the planned amount.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $490K-$670K | $687.0K | Above target |
| Conversion rate | 5%-7% | 8.53% | Above target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 4,500-6,500 | 5,819 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $78.1K | Actual $67.0K | 14.2% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 3 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$332.5K**
- Actual operating cost: **$67.0K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 332.5 / 67.0 ≈ 4.96**

## Products

Storage Bins generated the most sales, with $239.4K, and contributed $124.7K in profit. Its 52.10% margin was the lowest among the three products, and its return rate was 4.8%. Spin Mop had lower sales at $225.1K, but its $130.5K profit was the highest. It also had the strongest margin at 57.99% and the lowest return rate at 1.4%, making it the clearest product-level combination of profit and returns.

Hanger Set sold the most units, at 2,549, but generated $137.9K in sales and $77.2K in profit. Its 56.03% margin was above Storage Bins, while its 7.8% return rate was the highest. That return rate is worth reviewing alongside the unit volume before using the same presentation or sales approach in another campaign.

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Storage Bins | 1,129 | $239.4K | $124.7K | 52.10% | 4.8% |
| Spin Mop | 799 | $225.1K | $130.5K | 57.99% | 1.4% |
| Hanger Set | 2,549 | $137.9K | $77.2K | 56.03% | 7.8% |

## Lessons

Keep the Spin Mop demonstration and product explanation as a reference for future streams. Its profit, margin, and return rate were strongest among the products.

Review the Hanger Set returns before repeating its current sales approach. Its unit volume was highest, but so was its return rate.

The campaign finished below planned operating cost while exceeding the GMV and conversion targets. Preserve the cost controls that supported that result.
