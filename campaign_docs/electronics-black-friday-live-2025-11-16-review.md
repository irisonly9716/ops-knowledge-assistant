# Electronics Black Friday Live - Post-Campaign Review

## Overview

Consumer Electronics ran Electronics Black Friday Live from Nov 16, 2025 to Dec 12, 2025, streaming on 27 days. Daniel Bright hosted the campaign. GMV exceeded target, but conversion rate and peak concurrent viewers were below target. Operating cost was flagged for exceeding plan by more than 5%.

## Results

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $710K-$970K | $1.04M | Above target |
| Conversion rate | 9%-11% | 8.60% | Below target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 13,500-15,500 | 12,176 | Below target |
| Operating cost | Planned $154.2K | Actual $164.5K | **6.6% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$366.5K**
- Actual operating cost: **$164.5K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 366.5 / 164.5 ≈ 2.23**

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bluetooth Speaker | 1,895 | $298.1K | $124.0K | 41.62% | 10.5% |
| Wireless Earbuds | 2,683 | $251.3K | $118.2K | 47.03% | 3.8% |
| Smartwatch | 1,688 | $199.0K | $82.8K | 41.61% | 11.4% |
| Tablet Stand | 1,393 | $91.1K | $39.0K | 42.75% | 2.9% |
| Power Bank | 23 | $5.1K | $2.5K | 49.53% | 6.3% |

## What Worked

- GMV reached $1.04M, above the $710K-$970K target.
- Total product profit was $366.5K against actual operating cost of $164.5K, for an ROI of 2.23.
- Wireless Earbuds led unit sales at 2,683 units and had a 3.8% return rate.
- Tablet Stand had a 2.9% return rate, the lowest among the products.
- Power Bank had the highest margin at 49.53%, but sales were limited to 23 units.

## What Didn't

- Conversion rate was 8.60%, below the 9%-11% target.
- Peak concurrent viewers reached 12,176, below the 13,500-15,500 target.
- Operating cost was $164.5K against a planned $154.2K. The result was flagged as 6.6% over budget.
- Bluetooth Speaker and Smartwatch had return rates of 10.5% and 11.4%, respectively.

## Actions

1. Review the campaign’s conversion performance and identify where viewers dropped out before purchasing.
2. Review traffic and programming by stream day to understand why peak concurrent viewers missed target.
3. Investigate the operating cost overrun and identify which costs can be controlled in future campaigns.
4. Review returns for Bluetooth Speaker and Smartwatch and determine whether product information or live demonstrations need changes.
5. Assess whether to increase Power Bank exposure, given its margin, while considering its low unit sales.
