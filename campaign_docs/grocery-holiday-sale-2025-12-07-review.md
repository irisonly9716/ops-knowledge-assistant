# Grocery Holiday Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## How It Went

Grocery Holiday Sale ran from Dec 7, 2025 to Jan 11, 2026, with Fresh Grocery streaming on 36 days and Nicole Mack as host. The campaign finished in a strong position: GMV landed within the target range, while conversion rate and peak concurrent viewers exceeded their targets. The audience response was strongest in conversion and live attendance, giving the team a clear signal that viewers were willing to act on the offer and that the programming held their attention. Operating cost also came in below the planned amount, which helped the campaign deliver a positive ROI. Product results were not uniform, though. Organic Veggie Box and Instant Ramen led in sales and profit, while Fresh Cherries and Ribeye Steak posted higher return rates. Those differences matter for the next grocery event: the top-line result was solid, but product selection and return risk need to be reviewed together before the next run.

Nicole Mack’s campaign cleared its audience and efficiency goals without overspending.

## Results

GMV was on target, and conversion rate and peak concurrent viewers were above target. Operating cost was below plan, supporting an ROI of 1.62.

| Metric | Target (set in planning) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $1,750K-$2,380K | $1.77M | On target |
| Conversion rate | 4%-6% | 7.25% | Above target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 11,500-13,500 | 14,668 | Above target |
| Operating cost | Planned $352.6K | Actual $300.5K | 14.8% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$485.7K**
- Actual operating cost: **$300.5K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 485.7 / 300.5 ≈ 1.62**

## Products

Organic Veggie Box and Instant Ramen delivered the largest sales and profit contributions. Organic Veggie Box recorded $559.3K in sales and $189.9K in profit from 2,223 units. Instant Ramen sold 5,877 units and generated $418.2K in sales and $159.3K in profit. Their return rates were 6.8% and 6.0%, respectively, giving the team strong volume and profit with comparatively lower returns.

Fresh Cherries and Ribeye Steak had the highest margins, at 52.36% and 53.21%, but also the highest return rates, at 13.5% and 18.6%. Their profit contributions were smaller than those of the two leading products. Review the return reasons for both items before deciding how they should be presented or stocked in a future campaign.

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic Veggie Box | 2,223 | $559.3K | $189.9K | 33.95% | 6.8% |
| Instant Ramen | 5,877 | $418.2K | $159.3K | 38.08% | 6.0% |
| Fresh Cherries | 1,344 | $186.7K | $97.8K | 52.36% | 13.5% |
| Ribeye Steak | 403 | $72.9K | $38.8K | 53.21% | 18.6% |

## Lessons

Keep Organic Veggie Box and Instant Ramen in consideration for future grocery programming, given their sales and profit results.

Review Fresh Cherries and Ribeye Steak returns at the item level. Their margins were strong, but the return rates warrant follow-up.

Use the campaign’s above-target conversion and peak attendance as a reference when planning the next event, while keeping operating cost discipline in place.
