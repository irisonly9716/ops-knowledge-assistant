# Beauty Back to School - Post-Campaign Review

## Summary

Beauty Back to School streamed on 30 days, from Aug 9, 2026 to Sep 7, 2026, hosted by Jonathan Wilkerson.
GMV and peak concurrent viewers met target; conversion rate was below target. Operating cost was under budget.

## Numbers

Results against targets:

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $1,040K-$1,420K | $1.41M | On target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 6.99% | Below target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 15,000-17,000 | 15,667 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $233.3K | Actual $219.3K | 6.0% under budget |

ROI:

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$556.3K**
- Actual operating cost: **$219.3K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 556.3 / 219.3 ≈ 2.54**
