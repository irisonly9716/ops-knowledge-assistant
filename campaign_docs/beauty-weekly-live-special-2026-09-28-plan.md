# Beauty Weekly Live Special (Sep 28, 2026) - Campaign Plan

## Background

This Beauty campaign runs from Sep 28, 2026 to Nov 3, 2026, for 37 days. Jonathan Wilkerson hosts the live special. The lineup includes Foundation, Lipstick, Eyeshadow Palette, and Serum.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Foundation. It anchors the color cosmetics lineup and is included in the spend-and-save tiers.
- **Add-on product**: Lipstick. The free gift offer gives shoppers a reason to add it to an order.
- **Volume product**: Eyeshadow Palette. It is included in the spend-and-save tiers and can help build larger baskets.
- **Repeat-purchase product**: Serum. Its buy-two offer gives shoppers a reason to purchase more than one.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Foundation: No discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Lipstick: Free gift with orders over $40
- Eyeshadow Palette: No discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Serum: Buy two, get 20% off

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $750,000-$1,030,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 6,500-8,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $80,000

## Risks

- Spend-and-save tiers may be hard for shoppers to understand during a live segment.
- The free gift offer may create fulfillment issues if gift inventory runs low.
- The planned operating cost is about $80,000, so spending needs to be monitored against the plan.
