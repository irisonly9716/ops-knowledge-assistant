# Home Anniversary Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## How It Went

The Home Anniversary Sale ran from Sep 4, 2026 to Sep 24, 2026, with Ashley Garrett hosting across 21 streamed days. The campaign held its audience and conversion performance within target, and operating costs came in under budget. GMV, however, finished below the target range. From a producer’s perspective, the results show that the stream format and audience response were working, but that did not translate into enough total sales to reach the GMV floor. The product results point to a strong contribution from Spin Mop and Paper Towels, while several other products added sales at lower levels. This review focuses on that gap: the campaign delivered on important operating measures, but the sales volume was not sufficient to meet the overall GMV target.

The campaign was well controlled, but it did not reach its sales goal.

## Results

GMV was below target, while conversion rate and peak concurrent viewers were on target. Operating cost was under budget, supporting a positive ROI.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $800K-$1,090K | $766.1K | Below target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 7.96% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 9,500-11,500 | 10,978 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $89.2K | Actual $80.8K | 9.4% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 6 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$215.2K**
- Actual operating cost: **$80.8K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 215.2 / 80.8 ≈ 2.66**

## Products

Spin Mop led the listed products in units, sales, and profit. It also had a 52.13% margin, though its 19.9% return rate is worth reviewing alongside its sales contribution. Paper Towels was the next largest product by sales and profit, with a 17.6% return rate. Together, these products were the strongest sales drivers in the product mix provided.

Storage Bins had a 50.82% margin and the lowest return rate among the products listed other than Insulated Tumbler. Insulated Tumbler had the highest margin, at 59.50%, and the lowest return rate, at 1.9%, but sold 160 units. Trash Bags and Hanger Set contributed less sales and profit than the leading products, and their return rates were 18.6% and 17.9%, respectively. These results give the team clear differences to consider when planning product emphasis for a future sale.

## Lessons

Keep the operating approach that delivered on conversion, viewership, and cost control. The next review should focus on how the product mix and stream plan can close the GMV gap without losing those strengths.

Review return rates for the higher-volume products before repeating the same product emphasis. Use the margin and return results together when setting future stream priorities.
