# Electronics Holiday Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## How It Went

The Consumer Electronics holiday sale ran from Dec 6, 2025 to Dec 28, 2025, with Jennifer Brown hosting across 23 streamed days. The campaign delivered a steady run, with GMV within the target range and peak concurrent viewers also on target. Conversion came in above target, which points to a strong response from viewers who reached the stream. The result was not without a cost issue: operating cost finished above plan, and the overage crossed the threshold for a flag. That needs attention in the next planning cycle, even with the overall campaign returning more product profit than operating cost. The product results show different roles across the assortment. Bluetooth Speaker led profit, while Tablet Stand delivered the highest margin. Phone Case had the lowest return rate. These details give the team useful direction for assortment and presentation decisions, while the cost overrun should remain part of the read on the campaign rather than being lost behind the sales result.

The campaign performed well overall, but the operating cost overrun is the main execution issue to carry forward.

## Results

GMV and peak concurrent viewers met their targets, while conversion rate exceeded its target range. Operating cost was flagged because it finished more than 5% over plan.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $410K-$560K | $511.9K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 5%-7% | 7.71% | Above target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 6,000-8,000 | 6,272 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $90.2K | Actual $98.8K | **9.5% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$148.3K**
- Actual operating cost: **$98.8K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 148.3 / 98.8 ≈ 1.50**

## Products

Bluetooth Speaker was the strongest profit contributor, with the highest sales and profit in the assortment. Charging Cable also produced substantial sales and profit, while Phone Case combined a strong margin with the lowest return rate. Its return rate stands out as a useful reference when reviewing product fit and the way items are presented on stream.

Tablet Stand recorded the highest margin, and Power Bank sold the most units. Power Bank’s unit volume did not translate into comparable profit, so the team should review how it was priced and featured. Return rates were elevated across several products; check the product-level reasons before using volume alone to guide future assortment decisions.

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bluetooth Speaker | 845 | $86.9K | $41.4K | 47.62% | 19.8% |
| Charging Cable | 449 | $86.8K | $33.6K | 38.68% | 17.2% |
| Phone Case | 539 | $63.5K | $29.4K | 46.28% | 8.7% |
| Power Bank | 1,258 | $60.2K | $19.4K | 32.20% | 17.5% |
| Tablet Stand | 676 | $45.4K | $24.6K | 54.21% | 15.5% |

## Lessons

Keep the conversion result in view when planning the next sale. Review the operating cost overrun by line item and identify what can be controlled without weakening the stream.

Use the product results to guide placement and presentation. Protect the margin opportunity on Tablet Stand, investigate return drivers across the assortment, and assess whether Power Bank’s unit volume is producing enough profit for the space it receives.
