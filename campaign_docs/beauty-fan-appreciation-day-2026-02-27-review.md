# Beauty Fan Appreciation Day (Feb 27, 2026) - Post-Campaign Review

## Results vs Targets

The campaign met its GMV, conversion rate and peak concurrent viewer targets. Operating cost was 11.6% under budget.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $860K-$1,180K | $950.5K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 8.65% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 9,000-11,000 | 9,321 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $106.9K | Actual $94.5K | 11.6% under budget |

## ROI

An ROI of 4.15 means the campaign generated $4.15 in product profit for each $1 of operating cost.

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$391.9K**
- Actual operating cost: **$94.5K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 391.9 / 94.5 ≈ 4.15**

## Product Breakdown

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sheet Mask | 5,225 | $271.9K | $144.8K | 53.26% | 2.9% |
| Foundation | 1,038 | $264.1K | $98.4K | 37.25% | 12.7% |
| Serum | 2,023 | $243.9K | $135.7K | 55.63% | 18.4% |
| Eyeshadow Palette | 97 | $27.4K | $13.1K | 47.75% | 8.1% |

**Findings**:

1. **Sheet Mask led both sales and profit.** It sold 5,225 units for $271.9K in sales and $144.8K in profit, with a 53.26% margin and a 2.9% return rate.
2. **Serum had the highest margin, but also the highest return rate.** Its 55.63% margin produced $135.7K in profit on $243.9K in sales; its return rate was 18.4%.
3. **Foundation sold 1,038 units and generated $264.1K in sales.** Its 37.25% margin was the lowest among the listed products, and its 12.7% return rate merits review.
4. **Eyeshadow Palette had the lowest volume and sales.** It sold 97 units for $27.4K in sales and $13.1K in profit.

## Recommendations for Similar Campaigns

- Keep Sheet Mask prominent in the lineup; it paired the highest sales and profit with the lowest return rate.
- Review Serum and Foundation return reasons before the next campaign. Check whether product demonstrations and descriptions address common causes.
- Test ways to improve Eyeshadow Palette visibility during the stream, then compare sales and units with this campaign’s results.
