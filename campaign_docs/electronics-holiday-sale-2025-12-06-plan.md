# Electronics Holiday Sale - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

The Electronics Holiday Sale gives the Consumer Electronics team a focused way to serve holiday shoppers during a defined sales window, from Dec 6, 2025 to Dec 28, 2025. The lineup covers useful items people may be shopping for as gifts or for everyday use, with offers that are easy to explain and compare while the products are on screen. Jennifer Brown will host the campaign. The stream should feel helpful and steady, with clear demonstrations and direct explanations of what each offer includes. We should connect the products through practical needs rather than rush through a list: show how the speaker, charging cable, phone case, power bank, and tablet stand fit into common routines. Keep the offer details visible and repeat them when needed so viewers can make an informed choice without pressure.

The planned operating cost is about $90,000. Keep the execution focused on the planned lineup and targets.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup combines one straightforward percentage discount, two multi-item offers, and two products that participate in the spend-and-save tiers. Explain the conditions for each offer plainly; do not imply that the charging cable or phone case has a standalone discount.

- Bluetooth Speaker: 20% off
- Charging Cable: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Phone Case: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Power Bank: buy two, get 15% off
- Tablet Stand: buy two, get 25% off

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $410,000-$560,000
- Expected conversion rate: 5%-7%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 6,000-8,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $90,000

Use these targets to guide the campaign plan and team decisions.

## Risks and Mitigations

Offer confusion is a risk, especially where products are included in spend-and-save tiers but have no discount of their own. Jennifer Brown should distinguish the tier offer from a product-level discount and keep the explanation consistent on stream.

Multi-item offers can be misunderstood if the qualifying quantity is missed. State the buy-two condition clearly when presenting the Power Bank and Tablet Stand.

The campaign runs across a holiday shopping period, so keep the product demonstrations practical and the pace measured. Avoid suggesting that an offer applies beyond its stated terms.
