# Grocery Anniversary Sale - Campaign Plan

## Background

Our Fresh Grocery campaign runs from Sep 10, 2026 to Sep 30, 2026 (21 days), hosted by Jeffrey Chavez. The lineup includes Instant Ramen, Frozen Dumplings, Mixed Nut Gift Box, Ribeye Steak, and Snow Crab Legs. The plan uses spend-and-save tiers and free gifts to support order value, with Snow Crab Legs as a standalone promotion.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Snow Crab Legs. Its buy-two promotion gives the sale a clear featured offer.
- **Volume product**: Instant Ramen. It joins the spend-and-save tiers without a standalone discount, supporting orders across the lineup.
- **Add-on product**: Mixed Nut Gift Box. It is a free gift at a lower order threshold, giving shoppers a reason to add items.
- **Add-on product**: Frozen Dumplings. Its free gift offer supports larger orders.
- **Add-on product**: Ribeye Steak. It shares the higher free gift threshold and gives shoppers another option for qualifying orders.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Instant Ramen: No discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Frozen Dumplings: Free gift with orders over $80
- Mixed Nut Gift Box: Free gift with orders over $60
- Ribeye Steak: Free gift with orders over $80
- Snow Crab Legs: Buy two, get 20% off

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $730,000-$1,010,000
- Expected conversion rate: 5%-7%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 13,000-15,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $125,000

## Risks

- Shoppers may find the spend-and-save tiers difficult to understand alongside the free gift offers.
- Free gift availability may not keep pace with demand, especially for qualifying orders.
- The planned operating cost is about $125,000; changes to the campaign setup could affect spend.
