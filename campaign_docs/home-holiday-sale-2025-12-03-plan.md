# Home Holiday Sale - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Home Goods campaign for Dec 3, 2025 to Jan 7, 2026 (36 days).
- Host: Troy Nielsen. Planned operating cost: about $91,000.

## Products and Offers

- Trash Bags: 10% off.
- Spin Mop: 20% off.
- Storage Bins: buy two, get 10% off.
- Insulated Tumbler: included in the spend-and-save tiers; no discount of its own.
- Hanger Set: free gift with orders over $80.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $470,000-$650,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 5,000-7,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $91,000

## Watch-outs

- Confirm offer eligibility and pricing before the campaign starts.
- Make sure the Hanger Set gift and Insulated Tumbler tiers are clear to viewers.
- Track planned operating cost against the budget during the campaign.
