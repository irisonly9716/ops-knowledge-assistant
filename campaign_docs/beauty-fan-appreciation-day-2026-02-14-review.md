# Beauty Fan Appreciation Day (Feb 14, 2026) - Post-Campaign Review

## Overview

Beauty Fan Appreciation Day ran from Feb 14, 2026 to Mar 22, 2026, with streams on 37 days. Jonathan Wilkerson hosted the campaign. Results were on target for GMV, conversion rate, and peak concurrent viewers. Operating cost finished 7.5% over budget, which is more than 5% over the plan.

## Results

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $750K-$1,030K | $766.1K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 8%-10% | 8.74% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 5,500-7,500 | 6,496 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $144.4K | Actual $155.1K | **7.5% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$247.5K**
- Actual operating cost: **$155.1K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 247.5 / 155.1 ≈ 1.60**

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eyeshadow Palette | 4,808 | $507.4K | $199.3K | 39.29% | 9.4% |
| Sunscreen | 969 | $59.4K | $31.5K | 52.95% | 8.4% |
| Lipstick | 81 | $23.1K | $9.0K | 38.87% | 12.2% |
| Sheet Mask | 70 | $19.0K | $7.7K | 40.74% | 12.7% |

## What Worked

- **Core targets met.** GMV, conversion rate, and peak concurrent viewers were all on target.
- **Eyeshadow Palette led sales and profit.** It sold 4,808 units, with $507.4K in sales and $199.3K in profit.
- **Sunscreen had the strongest margin.** Its margin was 52.95%, with a return rate of 8.4%.
- **Campaign ROI was 1.60.** Total product profit was $247.5K against actual operating cost of $155.1K.

## What Didn't

- **Operating cost exceeded plan.** Actual cost was $155.1K against planned $144.4K, flagged as more than 5% over the plan.
- **Lipstick and Sheet Mask sold fewer units.** Lipstick sold 81 units and Sheet Mask sold 70 units.
- **Return rates were highest for Sheet Mask and Lipstick.** Their return rates were 12.7% and 12.2%, respectively.

## Actions

1. Review the operating cost variance and identify the cost drivers before planning the next campaign.
2. Keep Eyeshadow Palette prominent in the product plan, given its sales and profit contribution.
3. Review the Sheet Mask and Lipstick offers and return reasons before repeating their placement.
4. Retain Sunscreen as a margin reference and monitor whether its return rate remains low.
