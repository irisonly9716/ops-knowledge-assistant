# Grocery Fan Appreciation Day (Oct 28, 2025) - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

Team, Grocery Fan Appreciation Day is a chance to thank the people who keep showing up for Fresh Grocery and to make the department feel welcoming on stream. From Oct 28, 2025 to Nov 14, 2025, Alexandra Le should make the campaign feel like a genuine thank-you, not a hard sell. Keep the conversation relaxed and useful: explain what is in each offer, answer questions clearly, and give viewers time to decide. The lineup gives us a mix of everyday favorites, a seasonal item, and gifts that reward larger orders. We should connect those offers naturally to the products being shown, without making viewers feel that every purchase needs to qualify for a deal. Frozen Dumplings are included in the spend-and-save tiers, so describe that clearly rather than implying they have a separate discount. The goal is a warm, easy-to-follow stream that gives fans a reason to take part and makes the value of each offer straightforward.

Keep the tone appreciative and the offer details consistent across the campaign.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup should give Alexandra Le several ways to talk about the range, from discounted products to gifts with qualifying orders. Make the conditions clear before inviting viewers to buy.

- Frozen Dumplings: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Organic Veggie Box: a free gift with orders over $80.
- Fresh Cherries: 15% off.
- Mixed Nut Gift Box: a free gift with orders over $80.
- Snow Crab Legs: a free gift with orders over $80.
- Instant Ramen: 20% off.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $170,000-$240,000
- Expected conversion rate: 7%-9%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 1,500-3,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $35,000

Use the targets in the block to guide campaign execution and team check-ins.

## Risks and Mitigations

The three free gifts share the same order threshold. State the threshold plainly and confirm which gift applies before describing an order as eligible. Avoid suggesting that a viewer receives every gift unless the offer details confirm it.

Frozen Dumplings have no discount of their own. Keep the wording clear when discussing the spend-and-save tiers so viewers do not mistake participation for a product-level discount.

The planned operating cost is about $35,000. Keep execution within the plan and flag cost concerns through the normal team process.
