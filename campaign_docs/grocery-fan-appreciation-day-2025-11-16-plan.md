# Grocery Fan Appreciation Day (Nov 16, 2025) - Campaign Plan

## Background

Grocery Fan Appreciation Day is a Fresh Grocery campaign running from Nov 16, 2025 to Dec 19, 2025. Jeffrey Chavez hosts the campaign. The lineup includes Frozen Dumplings, Organic Veggie Box, Instant Ramen and Snow Crab Legs. Planned operating cost is about $126,000.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Frozen Dumplings. The buy-two promotion gives viewers a clear reason to try the campaign.
- **Volume product**: Organic Veggie Box. The multi-unit offer supports repeat purchases and basket building.
- **Add-on product**: Instant Ramen. It gives shoppers another grocery option and contributes to spend-and-save tiers.
- **Add-on product**: Snow Crab Legs. A higher-value option that can help shoppers reach spend-and-save tiers.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Frozen Dumplings: buy two, get 15% off
- Organic Veggie Box: buy two, get 10% off
- Instant Ramen: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Snow Crab Legs: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $760,000-$1,040,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 5,000-7,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $126,000

## Risks

- Fresh grocery inventory may be limited or uneven across products, affecting the lineup during the campaign.
- Shoppers may not understand how the spend-and-save tiers apply to Instant Ramen and Snow Crab Legs.
- Perishable products can be harder to fulfill consistently, so confirm handling and delivery plans before the campaign starts.
