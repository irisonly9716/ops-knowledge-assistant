# Grocery Summer Sale - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

Team, the Grocery Summer Sale runs from Jun 9, 2026 to Jul 18, 2026. We are using the campaign to give Fresh Grocery customers a clear reason to shop across a range of summer food occasions, from grilling and seafood to quick meals and fresh fruit. The lineup should make it easy for Nicole Mack to move naturally between those occasions on stream, rather than making the sale feel like a series of unrelated product pitches. Keep the offers easy to understand, show the products clearly, and explain how each one fits into a meal or an everyday shopping need. The tone should be useful and relaxed: help viewers compare options, answer questions, and make choices without overstating what an offer includes. The campaign is planned around operating costs of about $118,000, so keep execution focused and raise issues early if they could affect delivery.

The plan is to make the stream feel like a practical grocery shop, with clear transitions between products and offers.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup covers gifting, grilling, seafood, quick meals, fruit, and a product included in the spend-and-save tiers. Nicole should state the terms plainly when introducing each item, and keep the distinction clear between an individual product promotion and a tier-based offer.

- Mixed Nut Gift Box: buy two, get 15% off.
- Ribeye Steak: 10% off.
- Snow Crab Legs: 15% off.
- Instant Ramen: a free gift with orders over $60.
- Fresh Cherries: buy two, get 10% off.
- Frozen Dumplings: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $470,000-$650,000
- Expected conversion rate: 7%-9%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 3,500-5,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $118,000

Use the targets as the campaign reference during planning and execution.

## Risks and Mitigations

Offer terms may be confused, especially when a product promotion is discussed alongside the spend-and-save tiers. Keep the terms visible to the team and have Nicole distinguish the Instant Ramen gift threshold and Frozen Dumplings’ tier inclusion from the individual product discounts.

Product availability may change during the campaign. Check readiness before each stream and flag any issue early so the lineup and talking points can be adjusted without implying an offer is available when it is not.

The campaign spans multiple grocery occasions, which can make the stream feel disjointed if transitions are rushed. Group related products in the discussion and use simple transitions to keep the presentation easy to follow.
