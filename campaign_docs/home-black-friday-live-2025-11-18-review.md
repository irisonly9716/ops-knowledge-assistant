# Home Black Friday Live - Post-Campaign Review

## Overview

Home Goods ran Home Black Friday Live from Nov 18, 2025 to Dec 18, 2025, with Troy Nielsen hosting across 31 streamed days. GMV and peak concurrent viewers finished above target. Conversion rate was on target, and operating cost came in under budget. Product results were mixed: the Insulated Tumbler led on sales and profit, while Laundry Detergent sold 153 units and had a 17.9% return rate.

## Results

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $920K-$1,250K | $1.28M | Above target |
| Conversion rate | 5%-7% | 6.51% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 10,000-12,000 | 13,273 | Above target |
| Operating cost | Planned $200.2K | Actual $190.0K | 5.1% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 6 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$349.6K**
- Actual operating cost: **$190.0K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 349.6 / 190.0 ≈ 1.84**

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Spin Mop | 945 | $185.2K | $67.2K | 36.29% | 13.3% |

## What Worked

- Strong reach. Peak concurrent viewers reached 13,273, above the 10,000-12,000 target.
- GMV finished at $1.28M, above the $920K-$1,250K target. Conversion rate landed at 6.51%, within the 5%-7% target.
- Cost control held. Actual operating cost was $190.0K against planned $200.2K, 5.1% under budget.
- The Insulated Tumbler was the top product by sales at $198.0K and profit at $100.8K. Its 50.92% margin and 7.2% return rate were also strong.
- Storage Bins delivered $69.9K profit on $142.9K sales, with a 48.94% margin and 9.8% return rate.

## What Didn't

- Laundry Detergent had the highest margin, 58.15%, but results were small: 153 units, $5.9K sales, and $3.4K profit. Its 17.9% return rate needs attention.
- Return rates were also elevated for Trash Bags at 15.7%, Hanger Set at 15.2%, and Spin Mop at 13.3%.
- The Spin Mop generated $185.2K in sales, but its 36.29% margin was the lowest among the products with substantial sales.

## Actions

1. Review return reasons for Laundry Detergent, Trash Bags, Hanger Set, and Spin Mop. Use the findings to address product information, quality, or fulfillment issues.
2. Keep the Insulated Tumbler and Storage Bins in the strong-performing product mix. Review whether the Spin Mop’s margin can improve before the next campaign.
3. Assess whether Laundry Detergent warrants another live feature. Base the decision on its low sales and high return rate, not margin alone.
4. Retain the cost controls used in this campaign and document the format and hosting choices that supported results above target.
