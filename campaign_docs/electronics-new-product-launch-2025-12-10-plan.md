# Electronics New Product Launch (Dec 10, 2025) - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Launch Consumer Electronics products through livestreams from Dec 10, 2025 to Jan 5, 2026.
- Host: Daniel Bright. Planned operating cost: about $102,000.

## Products and Offers

- Wireless Earbuds: free gift with orders over $40.
- Smartwatch: 15% off.
- Bluetooth Speaker: buy two, get 15% off.
- Tablet Stand: free gift with orders over $80.
- Charging Cable: 15% off.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $640,000-$870,000
- Expected conversion rate: 9%-11%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 10,000-12,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $102,000

## Watch-outs

- Confirm inventory and offer setup before the campaign starts.
- Make order thresholds and multi-item terms clear during livestreams.
- Monitor planned operating costs throughout the campaign.
