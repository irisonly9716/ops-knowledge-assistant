# Home Back to School - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

Team, this campaign gives us a clear reason to bring Home Goods into the back-to-school conversation without making the stream feel like a hard sell. From Aug 3, 2026 to Aug 31, 2026, we will help customers get household basics in order as routines change. The assortment should feel practical and easy to shop: useful products, straightforward offers, and a host who can explain where each item fits at home. Lisa Hernandez should keep the pace warm and focused, showing how customers can combine products and understand which offers apply to their order. We should make the stream feel like a helpful planning session, not a list of promotions. The campaign runs for 29 days, with planned operating cost of about $48,000, so the team should keep execution disciplined and use the targets to guide decisions.

Our goal is a consistent experience across the campaign, with offers explained clearly before customers check out.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup pairs everyday laundry and cleaning products with storage and closet basics. Keep the presentation anchored in how these items can support a household reset. Be precise when explaining the order thresholds and the spend-and-save tiers; do not imply that every product has an individual discount.

- Laundry Detergent: a free gift with orders over $80.
- Storage Bins: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Spin Mop: a free gift with orders over $60.
- Trash Bags: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Hanger Set: buy two, get 25% off.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $190,000-$270,000
- Expected conversion rate: 8%-10%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 1,500-3,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $48,000

Use the targets as the team’s reference point for pacing and campaign decisions.

## Risks and Mitigations

The offers may be confused with one another, especially where a product is part of the spend-and-save tiers but has no individual discount. Lisa should distinguish product-level offers from tier eligibility whenever she discusses the lineup.

Order thresholds can also be misunderstood. State that the free gift applies with orders over the stated amount, and confirm the offer details before each stream.

The planned operating cost is about $48,000. Keep changes to the plan deliberate, and flag any execution issue that could affect cost or the customer experience to the campaign team.
