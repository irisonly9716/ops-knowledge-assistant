# Beauty Summer Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## Summary

Beauty Summer Sale ran from Jun 12, 2026 to Jul 21, 2026, with Derek Wright hosting across 40 streamed days.
GMV was on target and operating cost came in under budget; conversion rate and peak concurrent viewers were below target.

## Numbers

Results against targets:

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $1,090K-$1,480K | $1.37M | On target |
| Conversion rate | 8%-10% | 7.82% | Below target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 14,000-16,000 | 13,226 | Below target |
| Operating cost | Planned $250.4K | Actual $237.6K | 5.1% under budget |

ROI:

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$556.0K**
- Actual operating cost: **$237.6K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 556.0 / 237.6 ≈ 2.34**

Product performance:

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Facial Cleanser | 9,869 | $446.5K | $200.4K | 44.89% | 16.5% |
| Eyeshadow Palette | 1,683 | $387.0K | $185.5K | 47.93% | 5.1% |
| Foundation | 3,700 | $211.8K | $125.0K | 59.00% | 14.4% |
| Sheet Mask | 1,516 | $104.1K | $41.8K | 40.18% | 6.7% |
| Lipstick | 68 | $7.9K | $3.3K | 42.13% | 7.1% |

## Next Time

- Review the conversion rate and test changes to improve purchase completion.
- Assess how to increase peak concurrent viewers.
- Review the Facial Cleanser and Foundation return rates, alongside the stronger return rates for Eyeshadow Palette and Sheet Mask.
