# Home Summer Sale - Campaign Plan

## Background

The Home Summer Sale is a Home Goods campaign running from Jun 10, 2026 to Jul 24, 2026. Lisa Hernandez will host the livestreams. The lineup includes Storage Bins, Paper Towels, Laundry Detergent, Hanger Set, and Spin Mop. Planned operating cost is about $297,000.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Storage Bins. The standalone discount gives viewers a clear reason to consider the product.
- **Volume product**: Paper Towels. They are part of the spend-and-save tiers and can help drive order volume.
- **Add-on product**: Laundry Detergent. The free gift with orders over $40 supports larger baskets.
- **Add-on product**: Hanger Set. It also supports larger baskets through the free gift offer.
- **Volume product**: Spin Mop. The buy-two offer encourages viewers to purchase more units.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Storage Bins: 10% off
- Paper Towels: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Laundry Detergent: a free gift with orders over $40
- Hanger Set: a free gift with orders over $40
- Spin Mop: buy two, get 10% off

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $1,770,000-$2,410,000
- Expected conversion rate: 5%-7%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 10,500-12,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $297,000

## Risks

- The free gifts may add order value but also increase fulfillment complexity.
- Paper Towels rely on the spend-and-save tiers, so viewers may not respond if the offer is unclear.
- The planned operating cost may be difficult to manage if promotion uptake is higher than expected.
