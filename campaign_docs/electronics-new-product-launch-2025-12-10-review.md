# Electronics New Product Launch (Dec 10, 2025) - Post-Campaign Review

## How It Went

The campaign ran from Dec 10, 2025 to Jan 5, 2026, with Daniel Bright hosting across 27 streamed days. The schedule gave the team room to introduce the new products, repeat key demonstrations, and respond to audience questions over the course of the launch. GMV finished above target, while peak concurrent viewers landed within the target range. Conversion rate was below target, so the audience reached during the streams did not convert at the level we planned. Operating cost came in under budget, which helped the campaign deliver a positive return despite that conversion gap. The results point to a solid launch on reach and sales, with a clear opportunity to make product demonstrations and purchase prompts more effective. The product mix also mattered: sales and profit were spread across the lineup, while return rates varied enough to warrant different follow-up by product.

The campaign delivered strong sales, but conversion needs attention.

## Results

GMV exceeded target, peak concurrent viewers were on target, and operating cost finished under budget. Conversion rate was below target.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $640K-$870K | $887.6K | Above target |
| Conversion rate | 9%-11% | 8.76% | Below target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 10,000-12,000 | 11,085 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $101.8K | Actual $92.6K | 9.0% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$234.2K**
- Actual operating cost: **$92.6K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 234.2 / 92.6 ≈ 2.53**

## Products

Wireless Earbuds led sales and profit, with $151.1K in sales and $58.4K in profit. Tablet Stand sold the most units and had the highest margin, at 51.55%. Its return rate was 9.2%, close to Wireless Earbuds at 9.3%.

Smartwatch had the highest return rate at 19.8%, followed by Charging Cable at 17.2%. Bluetooth Speaker had the lowest margin at 31.00%. Review the smartwatch and cable returns to identify whether product expectations, demonstrations, or post-purchase issues need to be addressed.

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wireless Earbuds | 760 | $151.1K | $58.4K | 38.66% | 9.3% |
| Smartwatch | 510 | $144.4K | $55.6K | 38.50% | 19.8% |
| Bluetooth Speaker | 478 | $130.7K | $40.5K | 31.00% | 11.6% |
| Tablet Stand | 1,875 | $111.8K | $57.6K | 51.55% | 9.2% |
| Charging Cable | 253 | $49.3K | $22.0K | 44.64% | 17.2% |

## Lessons

Keep the sales and profit contribution of Wireless Earbuds and Tablet Stand in view when planning the next launch. Their results show that the lineup can contribute through different strengths: sales volume for the earbuds and unit volume and margin for the stand.

For products with higher return rates, check whether the stream set clear expectations and showed the main use cases. Use those findings to improve demonstrations and purchase prompts, with the aim of lifting conversion without increasing operating cost.
