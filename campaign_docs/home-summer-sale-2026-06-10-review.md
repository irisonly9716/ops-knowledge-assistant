# Home Summer Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## Summary

Home Summer Sale ran from Jun 10, 2026 to Jul 24, 2026, with Lisa Hernandez streaming on 45 days.
Conversion and peak viewership exceeded targets. GMV was below target, and operating cost was more than 5% over plan.

| GMV | $1,770K-$2,410K | $1.68M | Below target |

| Operating cost | Planned $297.1K | Actual $314.6K | **5.9% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |
