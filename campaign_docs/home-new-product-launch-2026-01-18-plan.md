# Home New Product Launch (Jan 18, 2026) - Campaign Plan

## Background

This Home Goods campaign introduces five new products through livestreams. It runs from Jan 18, 2026 to Feb 5, 2026 (19 days), hosted by Lisa Hernandez. Planned operating cost is about $113,000.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Paper Towels. The free-gift threshold gives viewers a clear reason to try the new product.
- **Volume product**: Laundry Detergent. A lower free-gift threshold can encourage more orders.
- **Add-on product**: Insulated Tumbler. Its multi-item offer gives viewers a reason to add a second unit.
- **Volume product**: Trash Bags. The accessible free-gift threshold supports order growth.
- **Add-on product**: Storage Bins. The multi-item offer can encourage viewers to buy more than one.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Paper Towels: a free gift with orders over $80
- Laundry Detergent: a free gift with orders over $60
- Insulated Tumbler: buy two, get 20% off
- Trash Bags: a free gift with orders over $40
- Storage Bins: buy two, get 20% off

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $460,000-$640,000
- Expected conversion rate: 8%-10%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 9,000-11,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $113,000

## Risks

- Viewers may not understand how the free-gift thresholds apply across products, which could slow purchase decisions.
- Multi-item discounts may not be compelling if viewers only need one tumbler or storage bin.
- New products may need clear demonstrations and explanations before viewers feel ready to buy.
