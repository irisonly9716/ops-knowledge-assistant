# Grocery Fan Appreciation Day (Oct 28, 2025) - Post-Campaign Review

## Results vs Targets

GMV, conversion rate and peak concurrent viewers all finished within target. Operating cost was 11.9% over budget, exceeding the 5% over-plan threshold.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $170K-$240K | $191.7K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 8.06% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 1,500-3,500 | 2,472 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $34.7K | Actual $38.8K | **11.9% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

## ROI

An ROI of 1.37 means the campaign generated $1.37 in product profit for every $1 of operating cost.

- Total product profit over the 6 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$53.2K**
- Actual operating cost: **$38.8K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 53.2 / 38.8 ≈ 1.37**

## Product Breakdown

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen Dumplings | 322 | $35.7K | $10.9K | 30.58% | 13.2% |
| Organic Veggie Box | 154 | $29.3K | $15.5K | 53.03% | 1.3% |
| Fresh Cherries | 218 | $26.8K | $15.4K | 57.52% | 10.4% |
| Mixed Nut Gift Box | 407 | $16.3K | $7.2K | 43.96% | 7.4% |
| Snow Crab Legs | 45 | $7.1K | $2.5K | 35.20% | 6.7% |
| Instant Ramen | 15 | $2.8K | $1.7K | 59.75% | 3.5% |

**Findings**:

1. **Organic Veggie Box had the highest profit ($15.5K) and a strong margin (53.03%).** It sold 154 units, fewer than Frozen Dumplings and Mixed Nut Gift Box, but delivered the most profit.
2. **Fresh Cherries had the second-highest profit ($15.4K) and the highest margin (57.52%).** Its 10.4% return rate is worth reviewing before the next campaign.
3. **Frozen Dumplings led sales ($35.7K) but had the lowest margin (30.58%) and the highest return rate (13.2%).** The volume did not translate into the strongest profit.
4. **Mixed Nut Gift Box sold the most units (407) but generated $7.2K profit.** Instant Ramen sold 15 units and had a 59.75% margin, but its sales were $2.8K.

## Recommendations for Similar Campaigns

- Review Frozen Dumplings return reasons and test changes to product information or on-stream demonstrations before featuring it at the same scale.
- Keep Organic Veggie Box and Fresh Cherries prominent, while checking Fresh Cherries returns and fulfillment issues.
- Review the cost drivers behind the 11.9% overrun and include an appropriate operating-cost buffer in the next plan.
- Test ways to raise Instant Ramen volume without weakening its margin.
