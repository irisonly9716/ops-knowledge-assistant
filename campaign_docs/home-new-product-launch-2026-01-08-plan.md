# Home New Product Launch (Jan 8, 2026) - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Launch Home Goods products through livestreams from Jan 8, 2026 to Feb 16, 2026.
- Keep planned operating cost near $78,000.

## Products and Offers

- Storage Bins: 25% off.
- Spin Mop: free gift with orders over $60.
- Hanger Set: no discount of its own; included in the spend-and-save tiers.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $490,000-$670,000
- Expected conversion rate: 5%-7%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 4,500-6,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $78,000
