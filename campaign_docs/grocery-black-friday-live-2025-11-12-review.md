# Grocery Black Friday Live - Post-Campaign Review

## Summary

The campaign ran on 28 days from Nov 12, 2025 to Dec 9, 2025, hosted by Jeffrey Chavez.
GMV and peak viewers met target; conversion was below target, and operating cost exceeded plan.

## Numbers

Results against targets:

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $170K-$240K | $181.1K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 9%-11% | 8.84% | Below target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 1,000-3,000 | 1,960 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $28.9K | Actual $34.0K | **17.9% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

ROI:

- Total product profit over the 6 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$68.6K**
- Actual operating cost: **$34.0K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 68.6 / 34.0 ≈ 2.02**

Product results:

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh Cherries | 224 | $38.2K | $20.1K | 52.62% | 1.5% |
| Frozen Dumplings | 378 | $35.3K | $12.2K | 34.61% | 12.6% |
| Instant Ramen | 122 | $25.3K | $14.2K | 56.16% | 1.4% |
| Mixed Nut Gift Box | 112 | $22.2K | $12.0K | 53.92% | 3.9% |
| Organic Veggie Box | 284 | $20.4K | $8.4K | 41.13% | 18.8% |
| Snow Crab Legs | 40 | $5.4K | $1.7K | 31.79% | 10.2% |

## Next Time

- Review the operating cost overrun and identify where costs can be controlled.
- Test changes to the stream and offers to improve conversion.
- Review return rates for Frozen Dumplings, Organic Veggie Box, and Snow Crab Legs.
