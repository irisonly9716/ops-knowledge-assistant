# Electronics Anniversary Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## Summary

The Consumer Electronics campaign ran from Sep 2, 2026 to Oct 6, 2026, streaming on 35 days with Peter Callahan Jr. as host.
GMV, conversion rate, and peak concurrent viewers met targets; operating cost came in under budget.

## Numbers

Results against campaign targets:

| Metric | Target (set in planning) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $190K-$270K | $227.8K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 8.92% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 1,500-3,500 | 2,172 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $25.0K | Actual $21.9K | 12.5% under budget |

ROI:

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$64.9K**
- Actual operating cost: **$21.9K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 64.9 / 21.9 ≈ 2.96**

Product performance:

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bluetooth Speaker | 207 | $60.2K | $19.9K | 33.10% | 12.4% |
| Smartwatch | 212 | $39.8K | $15.3K | 38.48% | 3.4% |
| Tablet Stand | 523 | $38.5K | $16.6K | 43.14% | 1.7% |
| Wireless Earbuds | 201 | $36.2K | $13.1K | 36.13% | 3.4% |

## Next Time

- Review the Bluetooth Speaker return rate before planning the next campaign.
- Use the Tablet Stand’s sales and margin results to inform product placement.
- Keep operating costs below plan while tracking performance against targets.
