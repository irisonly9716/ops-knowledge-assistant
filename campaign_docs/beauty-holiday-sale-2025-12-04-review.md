# Beauty Holiday Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## Overview

Beauty Holiday Sale ran from Dec 4, 2025 to Dec 27, 2025, with livestreams on 24 days. Derek Wright hosted the campaign. GMV and conversion rate met their targets. Peak concurrent viewers came in below target, while operating cost finished under budget. Product results show strong lipstick profit and a low return rate for Facial Cleanser.

## Results

| Metric | Target (set in planning) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $800K-$1,090K | $843.5K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 7.78% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 10,500-12,500 | 10,171 | Below target |
| Operating cost | Planned $143.2K | Actual $139.0K | 2.9% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 3 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$246.1K**
- Actual operating cost: **$139.0K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 246.1 / 139.0 ≈ 1.77**

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lipstick | 792 | $230.3K | $119.6K | 51.92% | 11.2% |
| Facial Cleanser | 1,597 | $195.0K | $67.3K | 34.50% | 3.0% |
| Serum | 837 | $168.7K | $59.2K | 35.11% | 9.3% |

## What Worked

- GMV reached $843.5K, within the $800K-$1,090K target range.
- Conversion rate was 7.78%, within the 7%-9% target range.
- Operating cost was $139.0K against planned $143.2K, 2.9% under budget.
- Lipstick delivered $119.6K in profit on $230.3K in sales, with a 51.92% margin.
- Facial Cleanser sold 1,597 units and had the lowest return rate among the listed products at 3.0%.
- Total product profit was $246.1K against actual operating cost of $139.0K, for ROI of 1.77.

## What Didn't

- Peak concurrent viewers were 10,171, below the 10,500-12,500 target range. The campaign met its GMV and conversion targets, but viewer count did not reach the planned range.
- Lipstick had an 11.2% return rate. Its profit and margin were strong, but returns are worth reviewing.
- Serum had a 9.3% return rate, higher than Facial Cleanser’s 3.0%.

## Actions

1. Review the livestream schedule and audience acquisition approach to address peak concurrent viewers below target.
2. Check lipstick and Serum return reasons. Use the findings to inform product presentation and customer guidance in future livestreams.
3. Retain the cost controls that kept operating cost under budget, and review product-level profit and return rates when planning the next Beauty campaign.
