# Beauty Back to School - Campaign Plan

## Background

This Beauty campaign runs from Aug 9, 2026 to Sep 7, 2026, for 30 days. Jonathan Wilkerson will host the livestreams. The lineup includes an eyeshadow palette, sheet mask, sunscreen, and serum, with a planned operating cost of about $233,000.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Eyeshadow Palette. Its back-to-school appeal gives the campaign a clear lead product.
- **Volume product**: Sunscreen. A direct discount can support steady sales across the campaign.
- **Add-on product**: Sheet Mask. It can help shoppers reach the spend-and-save tiers.
- **Add-on product**: Serum. It can complement the other products and help shoppers reach the spend-and-save tiers.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Eyeshadow Palette: 15% off
- Sheet Mask: No discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Sunscreen: 25% off
- Serum: No discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $1,040,000-$1,420,000
- Expected conversion rate: 7%-9%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 15,000-17,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $233,000

## Risks

- The spend-and-save tiers may be harder to explain than a direct discount, which could limit uptake for Sheet Mask and Serum.
- The campaign includes products with different promotion mechanics, so hosts should make each offer clear during the livestreams.
- Sunscreen demand may shift during the campaign, which could affect its role as a volume product.
