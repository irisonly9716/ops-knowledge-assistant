# Home New Product Launch (Jan 18, 2026) - Post-Campaign Review

## Overview

Home Goods ran the Home New Product Launch from Jan 18, 2026 to Feb 5, 2026, with Lisa Hernandez as host. The campaign streamed on 19 days. Sales and conversion met target, but peak concurrent viewers fell short and operating cost exceeded plan.

## Results

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $460K-$640K | $587.6K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 8%-10% | 8.82% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 9,000-11,000 | 8,250 | Below target |
| Operating cost | Planned $113.0K | Actual $138.3K | **22.4% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$207.8K**
- Actual operating cost: **$138.3K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 207.8 / 138.3 ≈ 1.50**

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper Towels | 1,290 | $175.0K | $85.8K | 49.03% | 10.3% |
| Laundry Detergent | 499 | $94.3K | $32.0K | 33.91% | 11.1% |
| Insulated Tumbler | 637 | $85.5K | $29.4K | 34.45% | 12.6% |
| Trash Bags | 460 | $59.8K | $31.9K | 53.30% | 10.6% |
| Storage Bins | 210 | $58.0K | $28.8K | 49.59% | 11.9% |

## What Worked

- GMV reached $587.6K, within the $460K-$640K target range.
- Conversion rate was 8.82%, within the 8%-10% target range.
- Paper Towels led product sales at $175.0K and profit at $85.8K.
- Trash Bags had the highest margin at 53.30%, with $31.9K profit on $59.8K in sales.
- ROI was 1.50.

## What Didn't

- Peak concurrent viewers were 8,250, below the 9,000-11,000 target.
- Operating cost was $138.3K against a planned $113.0K, 22.4% over budget. This exceeds the more than 5% over-plan flag threshold.
- Return rates were above 10% for every product. The Insulated Tumbler had the highest return rate at 12.6%.
- Laundry Detergent had the lowest margin at 33.91%, and the lowest profit at $32.0K.

## Actions

1. Review the operating cost overrun and identify which campaign expenses drove the difference from plan.
2. Check the viewer drop-off and promotion schedule to understand why peak concurrent viewers missed target.
3. Review return reasons by product, starting with the Insulated Tumbler.
4. Keep Paper Towels and Trash Bags in the product mix, and review Laundry Detergent’s pricing and costs before the next launch.
