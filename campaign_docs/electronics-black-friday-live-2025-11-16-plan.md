# Electronics Black Friday Live - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

Team, Electronics Black Friday Live is our chance to make the Consumer Electronics lineup easier to shop during the holiday season. The campaign runs from Nov 16, 2025 to Dec 12, 2025, and Daniel Bright will host. The plan is to give viewers clear reasons to consider several products, with offers they can understand while watching a live stream. We should keep the experience practical and calm: show how each item works, explain its offer plainly, and give shoppers time to decide. The stream should feel like useful product guidance, not a rush of deals. We also need to make the spend-and-save tiers easy to follow, since they apply to products without a discount of their own. Planned operating cost is about $154,000, so consistent execution and clear offer communication matter throughout the campaign.

Keep the pacing steady and make the terms visible as well as spoken. Daniel should distinguish product-specific promotions from the spend-and-save tiers.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup combines portable audio, wearable tech, and useful accessories. Demonstrate the products in a way that helps viewers understand what they are buying, then state the offer and any conditions before moving on.

- Bluetooth Speaker: buy two, get 25% off.
- Wireless Earbuds: a free gift with orders over $40.
- Smartwatch: buy two, get 20% off.
- Tablet Stand: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Power Bank: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $710,000-$970,000
- Expected conversion rate: 9%-11%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 13,500-15,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $154,000

Use the targets in the block above to guide the team’s planning and check-ins.

## Risks and Mitigations

Offer confusion is a key risk, especially when a product promotion and spend-and-save tiers appear in the same stream. Keep each offer’s terms clear, and do not imply that the Tablet Stand or Power Bank has a separate discount.

The free-gift offer depends on orders over $40. State that threshold accurately whenever the offer is discussed, and confirm the gift details before presenting them.

Live pacing can make explanations hard to follow. Repeat important terms, leave room for questions, and use the planned presentation flow so each product gets a clear introduction and offer explanation.
