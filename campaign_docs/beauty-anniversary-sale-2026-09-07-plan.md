# Beauty Anniversary Sale - Campaign Plan

## Background

Our Beauty Anniversary Sale runs from Sep 7, 2026 to Oct 8, 2026, for 32 days. Tommy Walter will host the campaign. The lineup covers color cosmetics, skincare, and sun care: Eyeshadow Palette, Sheet Mask, Facial Cleanser, and Sunscreen.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Eyeshadow Palette. A color cosmetics anchor that gives the campaign a clear featured item.
- **Volume product**: Sheet Mask. A straightforward skincare offer suited to driving order volume.
- **Core skincare product**: Facial Cleanser. A practical daily-use product that complements the Sheet Mask.
- **Add-on product**: Sunscreen. A sun-care item that can encourage customers to add another product to their order.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Eyeshadow Palette: Buy two, get 15% off
- Sheet Mask: 20% off
- Facial Cleanser: Buy two, get 10% off
- Sunscreen: A free gift with orders over $40

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $890,000-$1,210,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 14,000-16,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $211,000

## Risks

- The bundle offers depend on customers buying multiple units, which may limit uptake for shoppers seeking a single product.
- The Sunscreen gift applies only above the order threshold, so customers may not add enough to qualify.
- The planned operating cost is about $211,000; weaker-than-planned sales could make it harder to manage campaign costs.
