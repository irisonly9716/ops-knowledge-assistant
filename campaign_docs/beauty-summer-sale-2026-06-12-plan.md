# Beauty Summer Sale - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Run the Beauty Summer Sale from Jun 12, 2026 to Jul 21, 2026.
- Derek Wright hosts the campaign. Planned operating cost: about $250,000.

## Products and Offers

- Facial Cleanser: free gift with orders over $60.
- Eyeshadow Palette: buy two, get 15% off.
- Foundation: free gift with orders over $60.
- Sheet Mask: free gift with orders over $60.
- Lipstick: no discount of its own; included in the spend-and-save tiers.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $1,090,000-$1,480,000
- Expected conversion rate: 8%-10%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 14,000-16,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $250,000

## Watch-outs

- Confirm the free gifts are available throughout the campaign.
- Make the spend-and-save tiers clear, including how Lipstick qualifies.
- Check that the Eyeshadow Palette offer applies when customers buy two.
- Track operating costs against the planned budget.
