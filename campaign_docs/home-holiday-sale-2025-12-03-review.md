# Home Holiday Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## Summary

Home Holiday Sale ran from Dec 3, 2025 to Jan 7, 2026, with streams on 36 days, hosted by Troy Nielsen.
GMV, conversion rate, and peak concurrent viewers met targets. Operating cost was 21.0% over budget.

## Numbers

Results against campaign targets:

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $470K-$650K | $620.5K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 6%-8% | 7.05% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 5,000-7,000 | 5,298 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $90.6K | Actual $109.6K | **21.0% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

ROI:

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$203.2K**
- Actual operating cost: **$109.6K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 203.2 / 109.6 ≈ 1.85**
