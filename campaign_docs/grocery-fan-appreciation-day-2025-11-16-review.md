# Grocery Fan Appreciation Day (Nov 16, 2025) - Post-Campaign Review

## Results vs Targets

GMV and conversion rate landed within target. Peak concurrent viewers exceeded target, while operating cost finished more than 5% over plan.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $760K-$1,040K | $765.9K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 6%-8% | 6.50% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 5,000-7,000 | 7,249 | Above target |
| Operating cost | Planned $126.3K | Actual $155.6K | **23.2% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

## ROI

An ROI of 1.70 means the campaign generated $1.70 in product profit for every $1 of operating cost.

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$264.5K**
- Actual operating cost: **$155.6K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 264.5 / 155.6 ≈ 1.70**

## Product Breakdown

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frozen Dumplings | 772 | $213.1K | $106.9K | 50.15% | 19.6% |
| Organic Veggie Box | 1,684 | $193.6K | $59.7K | 30.81% | 9.5% |
| Instant Ramen | 1,047 | $147.3K | $84.8K | 57.60% | 2.4% |
| Snow Crab Legs | 141 | $29.4K | $13.1K | 44.55% | 4.7% |

**Findings**:

1. **Frozen Dumplings led sales and profit, with the highest return rate.** It sold 772 units, bringing in $213.1K in sales and $106.9K in profit. Its 19.6% return rate is worth investigating.
2. **Instant Ramen had the strongest margin and lowest return rate.** Its 57.60% margin and 2.4% return rate make it a strong candidate for future promotion.
3. **Organic Veggie Box had the highest unit volume but a lower margin than the other products.** It sold 1,684 units and generated $59.7K in profit at a 30.81% margin.
4. **Snow Crab Legs contributed the least sales and profit.** It sold 141 units, with $29.4K in sales and $13.1K in profit.

## Recommendations for Similar Campaigns

- Review Frozen Dumplings return reasons and address likely product or listing issues before featuring it again.
- Give Instant Ramen more prominence, and test whether it can sustain its margin at higher volume.
- Review the Organic Veggie Box offer and cost structure to improve its margin without sacrificing unit volume.
- Plan operating costs with the 23.2% overrun in mind and track spend during the campaign.
