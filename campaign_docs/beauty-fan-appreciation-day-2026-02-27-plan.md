# Beauty Fan Appreciation Day (Feb 27, 2026) - Campaign Plan

- Run a 33-day Beauty campaign from Feb 27, 2026 to Mar 31, 2026.
- Host: Jonathan Wilkerson. Planned operating cost: about $107,000.

## Products and Offers

- Sheet Mask: 15% off.
- Foundation: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Serum: buy two, get 15% off.
- Eyeshadow Palette: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $860,000-$1,180,000

- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $107,000

## Watch-outs
