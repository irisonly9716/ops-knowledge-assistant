# Electronics Back to School - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Run a focused Consumer Electronics campaign for back to school.
- Campaign dates: Aug 10, 2026 to Aug 31, 2026 (22 days). Host: Nicole Patterson.

## Products and Offers

- Power Bank: buy two, get 10% off.
- Bluetooth Speaker: buy two, get 20% off.
- Wireless Earbuds: free gift with orders over $40.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $620,000-$850,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 15,000-17,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $168,000

## Watch-outs

- Planned operating cost is about $168,000.
- Confirm each offer is set up correctly before the campaign starts.
- Keep an eye on order eligibility for the Wireless Earbuds gift and the quantity requirements for the discounts.
