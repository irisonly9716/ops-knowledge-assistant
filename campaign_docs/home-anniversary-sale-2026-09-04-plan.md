# Home Anniversary Sale - Campaign Plan

## Background

Our Home Goods campaign runs from Sep 4, 2026 to Sep 24, 2026. Ashley Garrett hosts the sale, which features Spin Mop, Paper Towels, Trash Bags, Storage Bins, Hanger Set, and Insulated Tumbler. Planned operating cost is about $89,000.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product**: Spin Mop. It anchors the spend-and-save tiers and gives the campaign a clear lead product.
- **Volume product**: Paper Towels. A household staple with a direct discount to encourage orders.
- **Add-on product**: Trash Bags. The free gift offer gives shoppers a reason to increase their order.
- **Volume product**: Storage Bins. A practical home item with a discount to support demand.
- **Add-on product**: Hanger Set. Its free gift offer can help lift order value.
- **Add-on product**: Insulated Tumbler. A discounted item that adds variety to the lineup.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Spin Mop: No discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers
- Paper Towels: 25% off
- Trash Bags: Free gift with orders over $80
- Storage Bins: 20% off
- Hanger Set: Free gift with orders over $60
- Insulated Tumbler: 25% off

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $800,000-$1,090,000
- Expected conversion rate: 7%-9%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 9,500-11,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $89,000

## Risks

- Spend-and-save tiers may be hard for shoppers to understand if the offer is not explained clearly during the livestream.
- Free gifts may run out before the campaign ends, which could affect the value of qualifying orders.
- Discounted products may draw attention away from Spin Mop and make the tier offer less visible.
