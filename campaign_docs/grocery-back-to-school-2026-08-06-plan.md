# Grocery Back to School - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

Team, Grocery Back to School runs from Aug 6, 2026 to Sep 1, 2026, and gives us a focused reason to help customers stock up as school routines return. Fresh Grocery can make practical meals and pantry staples feel easier to plan, whether customers are shopping for family dinners, quick lunches, or a busy week ahead. The campaign should feel useful and relaxed on stream. Jeffrey Chavez should connect each product to a real shopping need, explain the offer clearly, and give viewers time to understand what they are buying. Keep the conversation grounded in food and everyday routines. This is not a hard-sell event; it is a clear, well-paced shopping session that helps customers make confident choices.

We plan to operate the campaign at a cost of about $247,000. Keep the stream focused on the products and the customer questions that help explain them.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup brings together a meal-centerpiece option, a gift-ready item, and a convenient pantry staple. Move between them at a steady pace, with a clear explanation of each promotion before asking viewers to buy.

- Ribeye Steak: 15% off
- Mixed Nut Gift Box: a free gift with orders over $80
- Instant Ramen: buy two, get 25% off

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $860,000-$1,180,000
- Expected conversion rate: 8%-10%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 10,000-12,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $247,000

Use the targets to guide planning and review, while keeping the customer experience clear throughout the campaign.

## Risks and Mitigations

Promotion terms may be misunderstood, especially when viewers join mid-stream. State the offer plainly when introducing each product, and repeat the relevant terms before moving on.

Product availability can change during the campaign. Check inventory before each stream and avoid promising availability that has not been confirmed.

A rushed presentation could make the lineup feel like a series of transactions rather than useful grocery guidance. Leave room for questions and keep explanations practical.

The planned operating cost is about $247,000. Keep spending aligned with the plan and flag any issue through the usual operations channel.
