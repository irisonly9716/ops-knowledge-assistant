# Grocery Anniversary Sale - Post-Campaign Review

## Overview

The Fresh Grocery department ran the Grocery Anniversary Sale from Sep 10, 2026 to Sep 30, 2026, streaming on 21 days. Jeffrey Chavez hosted. The campaign delivered $769.5K in GMV and finished above the conversion target. Operating cost came in under budget, while product returns varied sharply.

## Results

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $730K-$1,010K | $769.5K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 5%-7% | 7.26% | Above target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 13,000-15,000 | 13,192 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $124.9K | Actual $119.4K | 4.4% under budget |

- Total product profit over the 5 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$227.8K**
- Actual operating cost: **$119.4K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 227.8 / 119.4 ≈ 1.91**

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instant Ramen | 2,990 | $180.7K | $87.4K | 48.39% | 18.7% |
| Frozen Dumplings | 688 | $130.9K | $66.6K | 50.92% | 13.3% |
| Mixed Nut Gift Box | 336 | $90.9K | $31.6K | 34.73% | 1.4% |
| Ribeye Steak | 336 | $79.0K | $30.0K | 37.93% | 14.1% |
| Snow Crab Legs | 262 | $39.4K | $12.2K | 31.00% | 15.6% |

## What Worked

- GMV and peak concurrent viewers landed within their target ranges.
- Conversion rate reached 7.26%, above the 5%-7% target.
- Operating cost was $119.4K against planned $124.9K. The campaign finished 4.4% under budget.
- Instant Ramen led the listed products in units, sales, and profit. Its margin was 48.39%.
- Frozen Dumplings delivered the highest listed product margin at 50.92%, with $66.6K profit.
- Mixed Nut Gift Box had the lowest return rate at 1.4%.

## What Didn't

- Instant Ramen's 18.7% return rate was the highest among the listed products. Its sales and profit were strong, but returns need attention.
- Snow Crab Legs had the lowest listed margin at 31.00% and a 15.6% return rate.
- Ribeye Steak returns were 14.1%. The result points to a need to review how the product was presented and fulfilled.

## Actions

1. Review Instant Ramen return reasons and listing details before the next sale.
2. Check Snow Crab Legs sourcing, fulfillment, and product presentation to address margin and returns.
3. Review Ribeye Steak return feedback and confirm whether product expectations were clear during the stream.
4. Keep the cost controls used in this campaign and track them against the next grocery plan.
