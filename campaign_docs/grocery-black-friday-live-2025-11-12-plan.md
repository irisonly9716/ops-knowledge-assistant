# Grocery Black Friday Live - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

Team, Grocery Black Friday Live gives Fresh Grocery a focused way to bring shoppers together around useful food buys and clear value during the holiday shopping period. From Nov 12, 2025 to Dec 9, 2025, Jeffrey Chavez will host a 28-day campaign that should feel practical, welcoming, and easy to follow. We are not trying to make every item feel like a major deal. The stream should help viewers understand what is on offer, how the gifts and spend-and-save tiers work, and which products fit their needs. Keep the pace steady, explain each offer before moving on, and make time for questions. Shoppers should leave knowing what they can buy, what qualifies for an offer, and when a product has no discount of its own. That clarity matters as much as the promotions.

Use a helpful, straightforward tone throughout. Make the value easy to understand without overpromising.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup combines meal staples, fresh items, and giftable products. Explain the terms attached to each offer plainly, especially where eligibility depends on the order total or on buying more than one item.

- Fresh Cherries: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Frozen Dumplings: buy two, get 25% off.
- Instant Ramen: 25% off.
- Mixed Nut Gift Box: a free gift with orders over $80.
- Organic Veggie Box: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Snow Crab Legs: a free gift with orders over $40.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $170,000-$240,000
- Expected conversion rate: 9%-11%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 1,000-3,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $29,000

Use these targets to guide the team’s preparation and keep the stream aligned with the campaign plan.

## Risks and Mitigations

Offer confusion is a key risk, particularly when an item is part of the spend-and-save tiers but has no discount of its own. Say that distinction clearly when presenting Fresh Cherries and the Organic Veggie Box.

Free gifts depend on the order total. State the qualifying threshold before presenting each gift, and avoid implying that either gift comes with every order.

The Frozen Dumplings offer requires buying two. Repeat that condition when discussing the discount so viewers do not mistake it for a single-item offer.

Keep the stream grounded in the planned operating cost of about $29,000. Stay focused on the listed lineup and promotions, and do not add claims or offers that are not part of this plan.
