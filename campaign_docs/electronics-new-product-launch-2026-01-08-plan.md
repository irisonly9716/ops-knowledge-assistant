# Electronics New Product Launch (Jan 8, 2026) - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Launch Consumer Electronics products through livestreams from Jan 8, 2026 to Feb 3, 2026 (27 days).
- Host: Jennifer Brown. Planned operating cost: about $92,000.

## Products and Offers

- Wireless Earbuds: free gift with orders over $40.
- Charging Cable: no discount of its own; included in the spend-and-save tiers.
- Power Bank: no discount of its own; included in the spend-and-save tiers.
- Bluetooth Speaker: no discount of its own; included in the spend-and-save tiers.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $570,000-$780,000
- Expected conversion rate: 7%-9%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 6,500-8,500
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $92,000

## Watch-outs

- Make clear that the free gift applies to orders over $40.
- Explain that the Charging Cable, Power Bank and Bluetooth Speaker have no individual discount.
- Confirm the spend-and-save tiers and which products qualify before each livestream.
- Track planned operating cost against the campaign plan.
