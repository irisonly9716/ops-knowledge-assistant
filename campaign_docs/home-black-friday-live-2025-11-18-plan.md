# Home Black Friday Live - Campaign Plan

## Goal

- Run Home Black Friday Live from Nov 18, 2025 to Dec 18, 2025 (31 days).
- Troy Nielsen hosts. Planned operating cost: about $200,000.

## Products and Offers

- Insulated Tumbler: no discount of its own; part of the spend-and-save tiers.
- Spin Mop: free gift with orders over $80.
- Trash Bags: buy two, get 25% off.
- Storage Bins: 20% off.
- Hanger Set: free gift with orders over $60.
- Laundry Detergent: 20% off.

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $920,000-$1,250,000
- Expected conversion rate: 5%-7%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 10,000-12,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $200,000

## Watch-outs

- Confirm spend-and-save tier details before the campaign starts.
- Check that the free-gift thresholds and offers are clear in the livestream and checkout.
- Track planned operating costs against the budget during the campaign.
