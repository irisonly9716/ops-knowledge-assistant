# Electronics New Product Launch (Jan 8, 2026) - Post-Campaign Review

## Results vs Targets

The campaign streamed on 27 days, from Jan 8, 2026 to Feb 3, 2026, with Jennifer Brown as host. Conversion rate was above target, but GMV and peak concurrent viewers were below target. Operating cost was 20.0% over budget, more than 5% over the plan.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $570K-$780K | $548.0K | Below target |
| Conversion rate | 7%-9% | 9.58% | Above target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 6,500-8,500 | 6,483 | Below target |
| Operating cost | Planned $92.0K | Actual $110.3K | **20.0% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

## ROI

An ROI of 1.38 means total product profit of $152.2K exceeded actual operating cost of $110.3K.

- Total product profit over the 4 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$152.2K**
- Actual operating cost: **$110.3K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 152.2 / 110.3 ≈ 1.38**

## Product Breakdown

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wireless Earbuds | 569 | $140.7K | $66.5K | 47.23% | 19.7% |
| Charging Cable | 887 | $100.2K | $36.3K | 36.19% | 4.2% |
| Power Bank | 288 | $72.2K | $41.0K | 56.81% | 16.2% |
| Bluetooth Speaker | 512 | $21.7K | $8.5K | 38.99% | 4.3% |

**Findings**:

1. **Wireless Earbuds had the highest sales and profit, but also the highest return rate.** Sales were $140.7K, profit was $66.5K, and returns were 19.7%. Check return reasons before using the same product pitch again.
2. **Power Bank had the strongest margin at 56.81%.** It generated $41.0K profit on $72.2K in sales, despite selling 288 units. It is worth testing more prominent placement.
3. **Charging Cable sold the most units, with a lower margin and the lowest return rate.** It sold 887 units at a 36.19% margin, and the return rate was 4.2%. It can support volume, but discounting should be watched.
4. **Bluetooth Speaker had the lowest sales and profit.** Sales were $21.7K and profit was $8.5K. Its return rate was 4.3%, so review its visibility and offer before deciding whether to feature it again.

## Recommendations for Similar Campaigns

- Review the operating cost drivers and set a tighter spend check during the campaign.
- Investigate Wireless Earbuds return reasons and adjust product guidance to address them.
- Test stronger placement for Power Bank while keeping Charging Cable as a volume product.
- Review Bluetooth Speaker’s offer and on-stream presentation before repeating the same approach.
