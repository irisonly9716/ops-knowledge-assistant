# Beauty Black Friday Live - Campaign Plan

## Background

Our main beauty campaign of the Black Friday season, hosted by Tommy Walter. It covers skincare (serum, sheet masks) and color cosmetics (lipstick), runs for about four weeks from mid-November, and has a larger budget than a regular campaign.

## Product Lineup

- **Hero product (first 20 minutes)**: Serum. Our best historical conversion in winter skincare; used to pull viewers in quickly.
- **Volume product (middle segment)**: Sheet masks. Sold in bundles to push units during the sale.
- **Add-on product (last segment)**: Lipstick. A gift-season add-on to raise the average order value.

## Pricing and Promotions

- Serum: Black Friday price plus a free travel-size sample; brings in traffic while keeping a healthy margin
- Sheet masks: 20% off when buying two or more boxes, to push volume
- Lipstick: spend-and-save tiers with the other products, no standalone discount

## Targets (for the post-campaign review)

- Expected GMV: $500,000-$700,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 5,000-7,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $71,000

## Risks

- Color cosmetics face heavy competitor promotions during Black Friday, so lipstick may underperform as an add-on
- Ad placement bids usually rise during Black Friday, so actual spend may exceed the budget
