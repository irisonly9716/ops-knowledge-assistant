# Electronics Summer Sale - Campaign Plan

## Why This Campaign

The Electronics Summer Sale gives the team a focused way to present useful consumer electronics during a defined summer campaign. From Jun 10, 2026 to Jul 14, 2026, Julie King will lead viewers through products that support everyday routines, from charging devices to listening on the go. The stream should feel practical, clear, and easy to follow. Explain what each product does, who may find it useful, and how the offer works before moving on. Keep the conversation relaxed and grounded in the product details. The goal is to help viewers make informed choices, not to create pressure or make claims we cannot support. With planned operating cost of about $183,000, the team should keep the presentation organized and make the promotion terms easy to understand throughout the campaign.

Use a steady pace and leave room for questions. Make the offer details consistent across the stream and supporting materials.

## Lineup and Offers

The lineup combines products with direct discounts and products offered as gifts with qualifying orders. Explain the threshold clearly wherever the gift offers are discussed, and avoid suggesting that a gift is included with every order.

- Smartwatch: 20% off
- Charging Cable: a free gift with orders over $60
- Wireless Earbuds: a free gift with orders over $60
- Power Bank: 10% off

## Targets

- Expected GMV: $1,730,000-$2,360,000
- Expected conversion rate: 6%-8%
- Expected peak concurrent viewers: 15,000-17,000
- Planned operating cost (placement fees + promotion + giveaways): about $183,000

Use the targets in this block to guide the campaign plan and team check-ins.

## Risks and Mitigations

The gift offers depend on order value. State that the qualifying order must be over $60, and make sure the wording is clear before viewers place an order.

Discounts and gift terms may be repeated across the stream. Use the approved offer details consistently, and check that product descriptions and on-screen information match.

A busy lineup can make it hard for viewers to compare products. Introduce each item clearly, explain its main use, and give viewers time to ask questions.

The planned operating cost is about $183,000. Keep spending aligned with the plan and raise any expected changes with the campaign lead before committing.
