# Electronics Back to School - Post-Campaign Review

## Results vs Targets

The campaign met its targets for GMV, conversion rate, and peak concurrent viewers. Operating cost came in 20.3% over budget, flagged as more than 5% over the plan.

| Metric | Target (see the campaign plan) | Actual | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV | $620K-$850K | $840.1K | On target |
| Conversion rate | 6%-8% | 7.86% | On target |
| Peak concurrent viewers | 15,000-17,000 | 15,275 | On target |
| Operating cost | Planned $167.8K | Actual $201.8K | **20.3% over budget**, flagged: more than 5% over the plan |

## ROI

An ROI of 1.30 means the campaign generated $1.30 in product profit for every $1 of operating cost.

- Total product profit over the 3 featured products, product cost already deducted: **$262.3K**
- Actual operating cost: **$201.8K**
- **ROI = total product profit / operating cost = 262.3 / 201.8 ≈ 1.30**

## Product Breakdown

| Product | Units sold | Sales | Profit | Margin | Return rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power Bank | 3,481 | $282.2K | $124.0K | 43.94% | 7.2% |
| Bluetooth Speaker | 1,141 | $279.2K | $94.3K | 33.79% | 19.4% |
| Wireless Earbuds | 745 | $143.0K | $43.9K | 30.72% | 1.9% |

**Findings**:

1. **Power Bank led on units, sales, profit, and margin.** It sold 3,481 units for $282.2K in sales and $124.0K in profit, with a 43.94% margin.
2. **Bluetooth Speaker had the highest return rate at 19.4%.** Its $279.2K in sales came with a 33.79% margin and $94.3K in profit. Check return reasons before using it as a lead product again.
3. **Wireless Earbuds had the lowest return rate at 1.9%, but the fewest units and lowest sales.** It sold 745 units for $143.0K in sales and $43.9K in profit, with a 30.72% margin.
4. **Operating cost exceeded the plan while all three campaign performance targets were met.** This supports reviewing cost controls separately from product and audience performance.

## Recommendations for Similar Campaigns

- Review the operating cost drivers and build a tighter spending check into the campaign plan.
- Check Bluetooth Speaker return reasons and address the main issues in product details and the live presentation.
- Keep Power Bank prominent, and test ways to increase Wireless Earbuds exposure while tracking its return rate.
